# GCC Payroll and Benefits Administration Agreement

> **Synthetic demo document.** All names, facts and figures are invented for retrieval-system testing. This document is not intended for execution and does not constitute professional advice.

This Agreement is effective 1 April 2026 between Seabrook Clinical Data India Private Limited, an Indian GCC controlled by Seabrook Clinical Data Pte. Ltd. (“Client”), and CoralLedger People Operations Private Limited (“Provider”). Meridian Harbor GCC Advisory Private Limited is the Client’s setup coordinator and has no payroll liability.

## 1. Term and employee population

The Provider shall administer payroll and benefits for the Client’s Chennai workforce, initially 145 employees and forecast to reach 320. The initial term is three years. It renews annually unless either party gives 90 days’ notice before the current term ends.

## 2. Included services

Provider will maintain payroll master data; calculate monthly gross-to-net pay; process approved variable pay and reimbursements; calculate tax withholding and employee/employer statutory contributions; produce bank upload files; generate payslips; prepare statutory payment worksheets and returns; administer annual tax declarations; coordinate joiners and leavers; enroll eligible employees in approved benefit plans; and operate an employee helpdesk.

Provider is not authorized to move Client funds, sign returns, make employment decisions, determine tax positions, provide actuarial advice or negotiate insurance coverage unless separately agreed.

## 3. Payroll calendar

Payroll is paid on the final working day of each month. The recurring timetable is:

| Event | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Joiner, leaver, attendance and variable-pay input | 8th calendar day |
| Employee tax and reimbursement documents | 10th calendar day |
| Provider preliminary payroll register | 16th calendar day |
| Client corrections and approval | 19th calendar day |
| Final bank and accounting files | 22nd calendar day |

If a deadline falls on a non-Business Day, it moves to the preceding Business Day. Late Client inputs are processed in the following payroll or an approved off-cycle run. Provider shall flag obvious anomalies but may rely on approved data.

## 4. Client approval and funding

Two authorized Client approvers must approve the final payroll register and bank file. The Client alone releases salary payments and statutory payments from its bank accounts. Approval confirms headcount, compensation changes, deductions and net pay, but does not waive correction of a concealed calculation error.

Client shall maintain sufficient funds and make remittances by law. Provider is not liable for penalties caused by insufficient funds, bank failure, rejected digital signatures or delayed Client approval.

## 5. Fees

The monthly fee is INR 275,000 for up to 200 active employees plus INR 750 for each additional active employee. Setup and historical-data migration costs INR 650,000. An off-cycle run costs INR 35,000; a Client-requested custom report costs INR 12,000 per report after estimate approval. Fees exclude GST, insurer premiums, government charges and third-party licence fees.

Invoices are payable in 30 days. Unit fees may increase annually by 5% beginning 1 April 2027. The stated contract value is an estimate based on forecast headcount.

## 6. Payroll accuracy and service levels

Provider targets:

- 99.7% calculation accuracy by employee line item;
- delivery of preliminary and final files by the calendar in 98% of cycles;
- Priority 1 payroll query response within two business hours;
- ordinary employee query response within one Business Day; and
- 95% employee queries resolved within three Business Days.

Errors caused solely by Provider shall be corrected in the same cycle where practicable, otherwise by an off-cycle run within two Business Days at no charge. Provider reimburses reasonable bank charges and statutory interest directly resulting from its error. Monthly service credits are capped at 10% of the monthly service fee.

## 7. Statutory administration

Provider will prepare calculations and filing support for tax deducted at source, provident fund, employee state insurance where applicable, professional tax, labour welfare fund and other agreed payroll obligations. Client remains the registered employer and is responsible for legal positions, authorizations and timely payment.

Provider shall maintain a compliance calendar and alert Client at least five Business Days before a scheduled payment or filing. Changes in law requiring material configuration or new services will follow change control.

## 8. Joiners, changes and leavers

Client shall provide approved employee data, tax identifiers, bank validation and compensation details. Provider will add a complete joiner within three Business Days. Compensation and bank-account changes require documented approval.

For leavers, Provider will calculate salary, leave encashment, recoveries, gratuity inputs and tax projection based on Client-approved separation data. Client determines entitlement and release date. Final settlement shall be prepared within five Business Days after complete inputs.

## 9. Benefits administration

Provider will transmit enrollment and dependent data to approved medical, life and accident insurers; reconcile monthly eligibility; administer additions and deletions; support claim escalation without adjudicating claims; and provide quarterly utilization summaries where available.

Insurers remain responsible for coverage and claims. Client selects plans and pays premiums. Provider shall not alter coverage or disclose medical details to Client managers except as lawful and necessary.

## 10. Data protection and security

Payroll data is highly confidential. Provider shall apply multifactor authentication, least-privilege access, encryption in transit and at rest, quarterly access recertification, logging, secure development practices and annual penetration testing. Production payroll files may be stored only in approved India-hosted systems unless Client authorizes otherwise.

Provider shall notify Client within 12 hours of suspected unauthorized access and provide continuing incident updates. It shall cooperate in legal notices and remediation. Subprocessors handling payroll data require prior written approval and equivalent obligations.

## 11. Data quality, records and audit

Client is responsible for accuracy and authorization of source data. Provider shall maintain processing records and payroll outputs for eight financial years or the longer statutory period. Client may audit controls and relevant records annually on 15 Business Days’ notice, and after a material incident. Provider may furnish independent assurance reports in place of intrusive testing where reasonably sufficient.

## 12. Business continuity

Provider shall maintain documented disaster recovery with a recovery-time objective of eight hours and recovery-point objective of four hours for the payroll platform. It shall conduct at least one annual test and share an executive summary. If primary systems are unavailable near payroll close, Provider will use the most recent approved data and a controlled contingency process.

## 13. Confidentiality and intellectual property

Confidentiality applies during the Agreement and for seven years afterward; personal data remains protected as required by law. Client owns its employee data, configured payroll outputs and Client-specific reports. Provider owns its platform, generic templates, calculation engine and methods. Client receives a licence to use delivered reports internally.

## 14. Indemnities and liability

Provider indemnifies Client against third-party claims and regulatory amounts resulting from Provider’s unauthorized disclosure, wilful failure to process approved payroll, or infringement by Provider technology. Client indemnifies Provider for unlawful deductions instructed by Client, inaccurate source data, employment decisions, or Client-selected benefit terms.

Neither party is liable for consequential loss. General aggregate liability is capped at fees paid in the prior 12 months. Provider liability for confidentiality or data breach is capped at three times that amount. Fraud, wilful misconduct, unpaid fees and infringement indemnity are uncapped.

## 15. Termination and exit

Either party may terminate for uncured material breach after 30 days, insolvency or material security violation. Client may terminate for convenience after the first year on 90 days’ notice. Provider shall complete any funded payroll cycle already commenced.

Exit assistance for up to 60 days includes data dictionaries, employee master, year-to-date balances, statutory registers, open-query log, benefit roster and secure historical reports. Standard export is included; custom conversion is charged at agreed rates. Provider shall delete residual copies after retention periods and certify deletion.

## 16. Governance and disputes

Payroll operations meet monthly and executives quarterly. Material changes require signed change orders. Provider is an independent contractor and may not bind Client. GCC Adviser may attend governance meetings but is not responsible for calculations or funds.

Disputes are escalated for good-faith resolution for 15 Business Days and then arbitrated by one arbitrator seated in Chennai, in English. Tamil Nadu law governs; Chennai courts have jurisdiction for interim measures.
