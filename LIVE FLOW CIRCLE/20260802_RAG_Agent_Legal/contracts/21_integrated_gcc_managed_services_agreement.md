The Client has established Northstar Biologics India Private Limited as its global capability center (**GCC**) in Bengaluru. The Client appoints the Provider to coordinate specified GCC support functions, and the Provider accepts that appointment on the terms below.

## 1. Definitions and interpretation

**Affiliate** means an entity controlling, controlled by, or under common control with a party. **Applicable Law** means laws binding on the relevant service or party. **Client Data** means information supplied by or processed for the Client. **Critical Incident** means an event classified P1 under Schedule 2. **Service Month** means a calendar month. **Service Levels** means the measures in Schedule 2. **Work Product** means reports, playbooks, dashboards, configurations, and other deliverables created specifically for the Client.

Headings do not affect interpretation. References to writing include approved electronic workflow records. An obligation to “ensure” a result is absolute; an obligation to “use reasonable efforts” is not.

## 2. Term

The Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for 36 months, unless terminated earlier. It renews automatically for successive 12-month periods unless either party gives at least 120 days’ written notice before the current term ends.

## 3. Services

The Provider shall deliver the following integrated services:

- HR operations, including employee lifecycle administration, policy queries, benefits coordination, and personnel-file controls;
- talent acquisition coordination for up to 120 hires per Contract Year;
- finance operations, including invoice validation, accounting support, reconciliations, and monthly management packs;
- procurement intake, vendor onboarding, purchase-order administration, and renewal tracking;
- company-secretarial coordination and maintenance of a compliance calendar, excluding reserved professional advice;
- workplace administration for up to 240 Bengaluru seats;
- IT service coordination, asset registers, joiner-mover-leaver tickets, and third-party service-desk oversight; and
- program governance, KPI reporting, issue management, and continuous-improvement planning.

The Provider shall perform services professionally, using suitably qualified personnel, documented procedures, and reasonable care consistent with experienced GCC operators.

The Provider may not bind the Client, sign contracts, release payments, make employment decisions, submit a regulatory filing, or give legal or tax advice unless separately authorized in writing by a designated Client approver.

## 4. Mobilization and service management

Within 20 Business Days, the Provider shall confirm the governance calendar, responsibility matrix, escalation contacts, baseline volumes, access requirements, and operating procedures. The first 60 days constitute the stabilization period. During stabilization, the Provider shall report open dependencies weekly; Service Levels apply for reporting purposes but service credits begin in month three.

The Provider shall maintain a service catalogue, procedure library, risk register, issue register, decision log, and action tracker. Material procedure changes require Client approval. The Provider shall not materially reduce staffing without 30 days’ notice and a continuity plan.

## 5. Client responsibilities

The Client shall provide accurate and timely data, access, approvals, policies, forecasts, and authorized decision-makers. It shall retain responsibility for corporate decisions, accounting policies, employment decisions, tax positions, legal interpretations, funding, vendor selection, and regulatory submissions.

The Provider is relieved from an affected Service Level only to the extent a documented Client dependency directly caused the failure, the Provider notified the Client promptly, and the Provider used reasonable efforts to mitigate the impact.

## 6. Governance

Operational leads shall meet weekly. A monthly service review shall consider volumes, Service Levels, risks, financials, audit actions, and improvements. A quarterly steering committee comprising one executive from each party shall decide escalated issues, material changes, and strategic priorities.

An operational issue unresolved for five Business Days escalates to the service directors. A P1 incident escalates immediately. A dispute unresolved for ten Business Days goes to the steering committee without preventing urgent mitigation.

## 7. Charges and payment

The Client shall pay a fixed fee of INR 1,900,000 per month, exclusive of applicable taxes. This includes the baseline volumes in Schedule 1. The aggregate indicative value over the initial term is INR 68,400,000.

Recruitment above the included annual volume is charged at 7% of the selected candidate’s annual fixed compensation. Additional workplace seats are INR 13,500 per seat per month. Approved project work is charged only under a signed change order.

Invoices are issued monthly in arrears and payable within 30 days. The Client may dispute an item in good faith within 15 days but shall pay undisputed amounts. Charges increase on each anniversary by the lower of 6% or the annual percentage change in India CPI for Industrial Workers.

Pass-through costs require prior written approval and supporting records. Travel outside Bengaluru is not included.

## 8. Service Levels and credits

The Provider shall meet Schedule 2. Monthly service credits are:

- 2% of the affected monthly service fee for one missed critical measure;
- 4% for two missed critical measures; and
- 7% for three or more missed critical measures.

Total credits are capped at 10% of the monthly fee and are the Client’s sole monetary remedy for an isolated Service Level failure, but not for persistent failure, fraud, confidentiality breach, data breach, or indemnified loss. Three misses of the same critical measure in six months constitute persistent failure and require a remediation plan within five Business Days.

## 9. Personnel and subcontractors

## 10. Information security and privacy

The Provider shall use role-based access, multi-factor authentication, encryption in transit and at rest, endpoint protection, annual penetration testing, background checks for privileged users, and logs retained for at least 180 days.

The Provider shall notify the Client within four hours after confirming a suspected security incident involving Client Data, preserve evidence, provide daily updates during containment, and deliver a root-cause report within ten Business Days. It shall not notify regulators or affected persons for the Client unless legally required or instructed.

The Provider acts as a processor where it processes personal data on Client instructions. It shall use data only to perform the Services, assist with data-subject requests, delete or return data at exit, and ensure confidentiality commitments from personnel.

Each party’s aggregate liability is capped at fees paid or payable in the 12 months preceding the event.
