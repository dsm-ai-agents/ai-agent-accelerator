# India GCC Recruitment Process Outsourcing Agreement

> **Synthetic demo document.** All entities, people, addresses, commercial terms and events in this agreement are fictitious. This document is intended only for RAG demonstrations and is not legal advice.

This Recruitment Process Outsourcing Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into on 1 February 2026 by and among Northstar Vector Systems Inc., a Delaware corporation (“Client”), its Indian setup adviser Meridian Harbor GCC Advisory Private Limited (“GCC Adviser”), and BluePeacock Talent Solutions Private Limited, Bengaluru (“Provider”). The Client is establishing a global capability center (“GCC”) in Bengaluru. The Provider will deliver the recruitment services described below.

## 1. Term and objectives

The Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until 31 January 2027 unless terminated earlier. The parties target 180 accepted hires: 110 software and cloud roles, 35 data and analytics roles, 20 finance roles, and 15 people-operations roles. The target is a planning assumption, not a minimum purchase commitment or employment guarantee.

## 2. Recruitment services

The Provider shall deploy one engagement lead, eight recruiters and two sourcing specialists; prepare role-based search strategies; source and screen candidates; coordinate technical and managerial interviews; collect compensation expectations; support offer preparation; and maintain an applicant tracker approved by the Client. The Provider may organize hiring events only with prior written budget approval.

The Client retains sole authority over job specifications, selection, compensation, offers, employment and rejection decisions. The Provider shall not make an offer, promise employment, change a job title or represent that it can bind the Client.

## 3. Requisitions and hiring plan

A requisition becomes active when the Client supplies an approved role description, location, grade, compensation range, interviewer panel and desired joining date. The Provider shall acknowledge it within one Business Day and present an initial slate within:

| Role class | Initial qualified slate |
|---|---:|
| Standard professional role | 7 Business Days |
| Specialist technology role | 12 Business Days |
| Director or scarce-skill role | 20 Business Days |

“Qualified” means meeting the stated mandatory criteria and completing the Provider’s preliminary screening. Delays caused by changed requirements, unavailable interviewers, compensation outside market range, hiring freezes or incomplete feedback extend the relevant target.

## 4. Governance and reporting

The parties shall hold a weekly operating review and monthly workforce steering committee. The Provider will report open requisitions, funnel conversion, ageing, source, compensation variance, offer acceptance, joining status, diversity indicators and reasons for decline. Candidate demographic data shall be reported only in aggregated form where legally permissible.

The GCC Adviser may review progress and facilitate decisions but does not become the employer, recruiter of record or guarantor of Provider performance.

## 5. Fees

The Client shall pay a monthly RPO management fee of INR 600,000, plus the following success fees for each candidate who joins:

- Grades A–C: INR 55,000;
- Grades D–E: INR 90,000; and
- Director and above: 10% of first-year fixed cash compensation.

The stated contract value is an estimate based on the planned hiring mix. GST and approved third-party costs are additional. Invoices are due within 30 days. No success fee is earned until the candidate’s first working day. Recruitment advertising, assessment tools and travel require advance written approval.

## 6. Candidate ownership and duplicates

A candidate is attributed to the Provider if the Provider first submits the candidate with evidence of consent and the candidate is not already in the Client’s applicant system or actively engaged by the Client during the preceding six months. The Client must identify a duplicate within five Business Days. If it does not, ownership remains with the Provider for nine months after submission.

No fee applies to an employee referral, direct applicant or candidate introduced by another agency before a valid Provider submission. The Provider shall not submit resumes without candidate permission.

## 7. Replacement guarantee

If a placed candidate voluntarily resigns or is terminated for documented performance or misconduct within 90 calendar days after joining, the Provider shall conduct one replacement search without an additional success fee. The Client must notify the Provider within ten Business Days and have paid the original invoice. The guarantee does not apply to redundancy, role elimination, relocation, material role changes, non-payment of agreed compensation, unsafe work conditions or Client breach.

If no suitable replacement joins within 60 days after the replacement requisition, the Client receives a credit equal to 50% of the relevant success fee against future invoices. Credits are not refundable in cash and expire at the end of the Term.

## 8. Service standards

The Provider targets 90% acknowledgement within one Business Day, 85% initial slates within the applicable timetable, interview scheduling within two Business Days after requested availability, and weekly tracker accuracy of 98%. Repeated failure in two consecutive months requires a corrective action plan. These are operational targets; the Client’s sole monetary remedy is a service credit capped at 5% of that month’s management fee.

## 9. Compliance and ethical recruitment

The Provider shall comply with applicable employment, anti-discrimination, anti-bribery, privacy and telemarketing laws. It shall not charge candidates, retain original identity documents, misstate vacancies or use discriminatory screening criteria. Background verification is excluded except for identity checks needed to prevent duplicate profiles. Employment verification will be performed under a separate arrangement.

## 10. Data protection and security

Candidate data may be used only for this engagement and accessed by personnel with a need to know. The Provider shall use encryption in transit, role-based access, multifactor authentication and auditable exports. Suspected unauthorized access must be reported within 24 hours. Unsuccessful candidate records will be deleted or anonymized within 12 months after the relevant requisition closes, unless consent or law permits longer retention.

The Provider shall not transfer candidate data outside India without the Client’s written authorization and a lawful transfer mechanism. The Client is responsible for its hiring decisions and privacy notices in its applicant system.

## 11. Confidentiality and intellectual property

Each party shall protect non-public business, workforce, compensation and technical information using reasonable care and use it only for this Agreement. Confidentiality obligations continue for five years; trade secrets remain protected while legally secret.

The Client owns role descriptions, interview materials supplied by it, candidate evaluation records created specifically for it and engagement reports upon payment. The Provider retains its sourcing methods, templates, market maps stripped of Client data, and pre-existing tools. Neither party acquires the other’s trademarks.

## 12. Personnel and subcontracting

The Provider remains responsible for its personnel and their wages, taxes, benefits and supervision. It shall not replace the engagement lead without notice and a qualified successor. Subcontracting candidate sourcing requires prior written approval; the Provider remains liable for approved subcontractors.

## 13. Warranties and disclaimers

The Provider warrants professional performance, authorized candidate submissions and reasonable validation of resume information. It does not warrant that a candidate will accept, join, remain employed or achieve any performance result. The Client shall independently assess candidates and employment eligibility.

## 14. Indemnities and liability

The Provider shall defend and indemnify the Client against third-party claims arising from candidate fees charged by the Provider, unlawful discrimination by Provider personnel, Provider confidentiality breach, or infringement by Provider-created materials. The Client shall indemnify the Provider for unlawful interview instructions, Client-supplied materials, or employment decisions made by the Client.

Except for fraud, wilful misconduct, confidentiality breach, data-protection breach, infringement or indemnity obligations, neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss. Each party’s aggregate liability is capped at fees paid or payable in the preceding 12 months. Liability for data-protection breach is capped at twice that amount.

## 15. Termination and transition

Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within 30 days, insolvency, bribery or unlawful processing. The Client may terminate for convenience on 45 days’ notice. On termination, the Client shall pay accrued management fees, approved expenses and success fees for candidates who join within 90 days after termination from valid submissions made before termination.

For 30 days, the Provider shall deliver the candidate tracker, consent records, open-requisition status and a secure export of engagement data. It shall then delete remaining Client data subject to law.

## 16. General

The parties are independent contractors. Neither assignment nor publicity is permitted without written consent, except assignment to an affiliate as part of a reorganization with notice. Notices shall be sent by courier and email to the contract contacts. Force majeure excuses affected performance but not payment for completed services.

This Agreement and approved statements of work constitute the entire agreement. Amendments must be signed. If a dispute is not resolved by the steering committee within 15 Business Days, it shall be finally resolved by arbitration seated in Bengaluru, in English, by one arbitrator under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. Courts at Bengaluru have jurisdiction for interim relief.
