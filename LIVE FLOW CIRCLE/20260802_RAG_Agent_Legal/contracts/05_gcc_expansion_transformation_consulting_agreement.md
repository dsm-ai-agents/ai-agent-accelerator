# Chennai GCC Expansion and Capability Transformation Consulting Agreement

> **Synthetic demo document.** All identifying and commercial information is invented. This contract exists only as a demonstration artifact and is not legal advice.

This Agreement is entered into on 4 May 2026 between **Saffron Current Transformation Partners Private Limited**, Chennai (**Consultant**), and **Caldera Grove Consumer Health S.A.**, Basel, Switzerland (**Client**).

## 1. Transformation objective

The Client operates a Chennai GCC with approximately 400 employees supporting finance, technology operations, reporting, and customer administration. It intends to expand to approximately 750 employees by December 2028 and establish higher-value capabilities in product data, digital commerce, clinical systems support, advanced analytics, intelligent automation, and global process ownership.

The Consultant will assess current maturity, design the future portfolio and organization, prepare function-migration waves, identify automation and AI opportunities, and support leadership alignment and change management.

## 2. Principles

The transformation will seek measurable enterprise value, responsible adoption of automation, continuity of regulated and business-critical services, meaningful career paths in India, clear accountability, and sustainable transfer of knowledge.

Recommendations will respect Client policies for quality, privacy, cybersecurity, medical and regulatory compliance, works councils, and third-party risk. The Client retains final decisions and accountability.

## 3. Scope

### 3.1 Current-state maturity assessment

The Consultant will assess strategy alignment, service portfolio, governance, talent, leadership, process ownership, technology, automation, controls, stakeholder experience, financial management, innovation, and culture.

The assessment will use document review, data analysis, interviews with up to forty-five stakeholders, six focus groups, and workshops. Findings will be rated on a five-level maturity scale and supported by observed evidence.

### 3.2 Capability portfolio

The Consultant will evaluate up to twenty-five candidate capabilities using strategic relevance, talent availability, process maturity, regulatory sensitivity, knowledge-transfer effort, technology dependency, value potential, and operational risk.

The Consultant will recommend capabilities to migrate, expand, retain, outsource, or defer. The recommendation is advisory and does not constitute a directive to transfer employment or regulated accountability.

### 3.3 Future operating model

The design will address global-to-GCC governance, functional ownership, product and process models, leadership layers, spans of control, shared platforms, innovation governance, funding, service measurement, and stakeholder forums.

The Consultant will draft role charters for the India Site Leader and up to twelve senior capability leaders. It will also propose a talent architecture, learning pathways, mobility options, and critical-skill succession approach.

### 3.4 Migration roadmap

The Consultant will define up to four migration waves through December 2028. Each wave will identify scope, sending and receiving owners, entry criteria, knowledge-transfer approach, parallel run, control validation, exit criteria, risks, and target headcount.

The preliminary target is 500 employees by June 2027, 625 by June 2028, and 750 by December 2028. These are planning assumptions rather than recruitment guarantees.

### 3.5 Automation and AI opportunity assessment

The Consultant will identify and prioritize opportunities in reporting, service operations, knowledge management, testing, document processing, forecasting, and employee support. Each prioritized use case will include expected value, data needs, human oversight, risk classification, dependencies, and pilot recommendation.

The Consultant will not deploy production AI, train models on personal or regulated data, or make automated employment or medical decisions. Any pilot implementation requires separate approval, security review, and applicable governance.

### 3.6 Change management

The Consultant will prepare stakeholder segmentation, communications themes, leadership alignment sessions, change-impact assessments, adoption measures, and a change-champion model. The Client approves all employee and external communications.

## 5. Outcomes and measures

The Parties will establish baselines during the first eight weeks. Candidate measures include:

- percentage of GCC roles in product, analytics, automation, and global ownership;
- time to transition a capability;
- stakeholder satisfaction;
- critical-role internal fill rate;
- employee regrettable attrition;
- automation hours released;
- process quality and control exceptions;
- number of global process or product ownership mandates; and
- run-rate value enabled by approved initiatives.

The Consultant will propose measurement methods but does not guarantee a particular financial, hiring, productivity, or adoption result. Benefits depend on Client implementation.

## 6. Governance

An Executive Transformation Council, chaired by the Client’s Group Chief Digital Officer, will meet monthly. It will approve portfolio decisions, migration waves, organization principles, high-risk AI use cases, and material changes.

The Transformation Management Office will meet weekly. Workstream meetings will occur as needed. The Consultant will maintain a decision log, risk and dependency register, benefits tracker, and integrated roadmap.

Critical risks involving patient safety, regulated data, cybersecurity, employment, or service continuity will be escalated within one business day.

## 7. Personnel

The Consultant will provide a Partner Sponsor, full-time Transformation Director, two workstream leads, two consultants, and part-time talent, finance, process, and responsible-AI specialists.

Named personnel may be replaced by personnel of comparable capability. The Consultant will conduct reasonable background screening and ensure confidentiality and security commitments.

## 8. Client responsibilities

The Client will appoint empowered global and India sponsors; provide strategy, organization, workforce, cost, service, process, technology, control, and stakeholder data; schedule interviews; provide system access where necessary; validate baselines; and decide recommendations promptly.

The Client is responsible for employee consultation, regulatory assessment, labor-law compliance, data lawful basis, implementation funding, hiring, and operational decisions.

Client delays or inaccurate data may affect findings and dates. The Consultant will document material assumptions and request confirmation.

## 9. Fees

The fixed fee is **USD 1,850,000**, exclusive of taxes:

- 15% on mobilization;
- 20% on current-state maturity report;
- 20% on capability portfolio and future operating model;
- 20% on migration roadmap;
- 15% on automation and change plans; and
- 10% on final closure.

The fee includes up to twelve trips between Consultant Indian offices and Chennai and two Consultant trips to Switzerland for executive workshops. Other travel requires pre-approval and is billed at cost.

Invoices are payable within thirty days. The Client will pay undisputed amounts notwithstanding a dispute. No fee is contingent on workforce reductions, vendor selection, or adoption of a particular recommendation.

## 10. Optional extension

The Client may request up to six months of implementation advisory support after 30 April 2027. Scope, staffing, and fees must be agreed at least forty-five days before commencement. The extension is not automatic and may not exceed USD 720,000 without a formal amendment.

## 11. Change control

Changes to assessed functions, number of capabilities, workshops, countries, migration waves, implementation support, or AI pilot work require a written change request. The Consultant will state the fee and timing effect. Only authorized signatories may approve a material change.

## 12. Acceptance

The Client has eight business days to review each milestone deliverable and provide consolidated, specific comments. A deliverable is accepted upon written approval, use for an executive decision, or expiry of the review period without material rejection.

The Consultant will correct a genuine material failure against agreed requirements. Additional analysis caused by changed strategy, new data, or preference is changed scope.

## 13. Confidentiality

Each Party will protect Confidential Information with no less than reasonable care and use it only for this Agreement. Access is limited to personnel, affiliates, and advisers who need it and are bound by comparable duties.

Confidential Information excludes information proven to be lawfully known, independently developed, publicly available without breach, or obtained without restriction. Compelled disclosures are permitted after notice where lawful.

Obligations last seven years. Trade secrets, personal data, product-development information, and regulated information remain protected as long as law or their nature requires.

## 14. Data protection and regulated information

The Consultant will minimize personal data and prefer aggregated workforce information. Identifiable employee data will be processed only under documented instructions, with access controls, encryption, and defined retention.

The Client will not provide patient-identifiable data, clinical trial subject data, or production health records. If such data becomes necessary, the Parties must execute a specific data-processing and security schedule before access.

A confirmed incident affecting Client data will be notified within twenty-four hours, followed by reasonable investigation updates. The Consultant will cooperate with the Client’s response and regulatory assessment.

## 15. Responsible AI

AI-related recommendations must account for privacy, security, accuracy, explainability, bias, human oversight, intellectual property, recordkeeping, and applicable Client policy. High-risk uses require approval by the Client’s designated governance body.

The Consultant will not enter Client Confidential Information into public generative-AI services. Approved enterprise tools may be used only within authorized configurations and documented controls.

## 16. Intellectual property

The Client retains all Client data, brands, policies, systems, and pre-existing materials. The Consultant retains its methodologies, maturity models, benchmarks, templates, software, accelerators, and generalized know-how.

Upon full payment, the Client owns Client-specific deliverables, excluding embedded Consultant materials. The Consultant grants a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free internal-use licence to embedded materials necessary to use and adapt the deliverables within the Client group.

Neither Party may publicly disclose transformation findings or identify the other without written consent.

## 17. Independence

The Consultant is an independent contractor and has no authority to bind the Client, hire or dismiss Client employees, approve organization changes, commit budgets, or represent the Client before regulators.

The Consultant will disclose material conflicts and commercial relationships with vendors it recommends. It may serve other consumer-health organizations with appropriate information barriers.

## 18. Warranties and compliance

The Consultant warrants reasonable professional skill and care and that deliverables will materially conform to the agreed scope. It will reperform defective Services reported within thirty days.

Each Party will comply with applicable anti-corruption, sanctions, competition, employment, privacy, and export-control law. The Client is responsible for determining whether recommendations comply with regulated-health, tax, employment, and corporate requirements before implementation.

No forecast, benchmark, maturity rating, or recommendation is a guarantee.

## 19. Indemnities

The Client will indemnify the Consultant against third-party claims arising from Client data, products, regulated decisions, unlawful instructions, or implementation materially inconsistent with the deliverables.

## 20. Liability

Aggregate liability is capped at fees paid or payable under the Agreement. Confidentiality and data liability is capped at twice the total fees.

The Consultant is not responsible for Client implementation, workforce actions, vendor performance, or regulatory outcomes except to the extent directly caused by its breach.

## 21. Termination

Either Party may terminate material breach not cured within twenty days, or immediately for insolvency, bribery, deliberate misuse of data, or an unremedied critical security violation.

The Client may terminate for convenience on thirty days’ notice. It will pay completed milestones, proportionate work in progress, approved expenses, and unavoidable commitments. If terminated before completion of the maturity report, a demobilization charge of USD 75,000 applies.

The Consultant will provide current deliverables, registers, and a reasonable handover. Additional transition services are charged at agreed rates.

## 22. Non-solicitation
