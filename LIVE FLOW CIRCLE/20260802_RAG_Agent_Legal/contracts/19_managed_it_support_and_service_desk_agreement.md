# Managed IT Support and Service Desk Agreement

> **Synthetic demo document.** The parties, personnel, systems, amounts and incidents described here are fictitious. This agreement exists only for RAG and contract-analysis demonstrations and is not legal advice.

This Agreement is effective 1 November 2026 between NimbusDesk Digital Services Private Limited (“Provider”) and Argent Vale Payments India Private Limited (“Client”) for end-user technology services supporting Client’s Hyderabad global capability centre.

## 1. Scope

Provider shall operate a service desk, deskside support and endpoint operations for up to 500 Client users. Services include:

- telephone, portal, chat and email intake;
- incident classification, troubleshooting and escalation;
- laptop and peripheral deployment;
- approved software installation;
- mobile-device and endpoint-management administration;
- joiner, mover and leaver (“JML”) execution;
- meeting-room technology support;
- device inventory and warranty coordination;
- knowledge-base maintenance; and
- monthly service reporting.

Application development, production-cloud operations, security monitoring, major network engineering, procurement cost and support for unapproved personal devices are excluded unless added by change order.

## 2. Service hours

The service desk operates 24x7 every day. Onsite deskside support operates 7:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. Monday through Friday and 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. on weekends and Indian public holidays. Provider shall maintain an on-call engineer outside onsite hours for Priority 1 incidents.

English is the primary support language. Hindi and Telugu support shall be available during normal Indian business hours.

## 3. Transition

The first 45 days are the transition period. Provider shall validate asset inventory, connect ticketing channels, document escalation paths, establish reporting, train personnel and accept operational responsibility.

Client shall provide policies, system access, current inventory, licence entitlements and subject-matter experts. Provider shall submit a transition plan within five business days and a readiness report before go-live. Service-level credits begin after the first 15 days of live operations, but security and confidentiality obligations apply immediately.

## 4. Incident priorities and targets

| Priority | Example | Response | Restore or workaround | Target resolution |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| P1 Critical | Site-wide outage; critical security tool unavailable | 10 minutes | 60 minutes | 4 hours |
| P2 High | Department-wide degradation; executive device failure | 20 minutes | 4 hours | 8 hours |
| P3 Medium | Single-user business-impacting issue | 2 business hours | 1 business day | 2 business days |
| P4 Low | Information request or minor inconvenience | 4 business hours | Not applicable | 5 business days |

Resolution clocks pause while Provider reasonably awaits requested Client information, user availability, third-party carrier action or approved change. Provider must document each pause. A ticket may not be downgraded merely to avoid a breach.

For P1 incidents, Provider shall open a bridge within 15 minutes, update Client every 30 minutes, and deliver a preliminary root-cause analysis within two business days and final report within five.

## 5. Service-level measurement

Targets are measured monthly. Provider shall meet:

- 95% of P1 and P2 response targets;
- 92% of applicable restore targets;
- 90% of P3 resolution targets;
- at least 75% first-contact resolution for eligible tickets;
- at least 4.3 out of 5 average user satisfaction; and
- at least 98% accurate device inventory.

Planned maintenance, documented Client delay and force majeure are excluded. Provider shall supply ticket-level measurement data, exclusions and calculation methods with the monthly report.

## 6. Service credits

A miss of one key target generates a credit of 2% of the monthly fee; two targets, 5%; three or more, 8%. A P1 restore miss exceeding four hours creates an additional 2% credit. Total credits are capped at 15% of the monthly fee.

Credits shall appear automatically on the next invoice; Client need not submit a claim. Credits do not relieve Provider from remediation and do not limit Client’s termination rights for chronic failure. Chronic failure means missing the same key target in three consecutive months or four months in a rolling six-month period.

## 7. JML services

For a standard joiner request received with complete approvals at least three business days before start, Provider shall prepare a compliant device, create authorised support records and coordinate collection before 10:00 a.m. on the start date. Provider may create accounts or entitlements only from approved workflows; business-system owners retain approval authority.

Leaver requests classified as involuntary or high risk shall be executed at the specified time with up to 15 minutes’ tolerance. Standard leavers shall be processed within four hours. Provider shall preserve equipment, document return and confirm disablement steps.

Provider shall not retain access merely for convenience and shall review privileged support accounts quarterly.

## 8. Endpoint and asset management

Provider shall maintain an inventory containing custodian, device identifier, serial number, location, encryption status, warranty and lifecycle state. Monthly physical or electronic reconciliation shall cover at least 20% of assets, with a complete reconciliation annually.

Provider shall deploy approved critical security patches within seven calendar days and other approved patches within 30 days, subject to Client testing and freeze periods. Provider may isolate a device presenting an immediate verified threat and shall notify Client without delay.

Lost or stolen devices shall be reported to Client’s security team within 30 minutes after Provider learns of the event. Provider shall initiate approved remote lock or wipe procedures.

## 9. Personnel, location and screening

Provider shall maintain at least one service delivery manager, six service-desk analysts and four onsite engineers for baseline demand. Personnel shall undergo identity, employment, education and criminal-record checks to the extent lawful.

Services and Client data shall be delivered from India. Remote support from another country requires prior written approval and appropriate safeguards. Provider shall replace personnel who lose required access or present a substantiated security or conduct concern.

## 10. Security

Provider shall follow least privilege, multi-factor authentication, secure administrator workstations, session logging and Client change-management procedures. Shared accounts are prohibited unless technically unavoidable, formally approved and individually traceable.

Provider shall notify Client’s security operations centre within 30 minutes of suspected unauthorised privileged access, credential compromise, malicious code introduced through support activity, or loss of a device containing Client data. Provider shall preserve evidence and cooperate with investigation.

Provider may not use Client tickets, logs or content to train a public or shared artificial-intelligence model. Any diagnostic AI tool requires written approval and shall not retain Client data beyond the support purpose.

## 11. Data handling

Ticket data may contain employee identifiers, contact information and technical logs. Provider shall process it only to deliver services, restrict access, encrypt data in transit and at rest, and retain closed-ticket data for 24 months unless Client instructs otherwise.

Provider shall return Client data in a commonly readable format and delete remaining copies within 45 days after termination, subject to legally required archival copies.

## 12. Fees

Client shall pay INR 2,600,000 per month for up to 500 supported users and 650 managed devices. Each additional active user costs INR 3,200 per month and each additional device beyond the included ratio costs INR 900 per month.

The maximum base value for the initial 36-month term is INR 93,600,000, excluding taxes, approved projects, procurement and volume overages. Fees increase by 5% on each anniversary. Invoices are payable within 30 days.

After-hours project work is included when responding to incidents. Planned migrations or events outside scope are billed only through an approved statement of work.

## 13. Governance

Operational reviews occur weekly for the first 90 days and monthly thereafter. Quarterly executive reviews shall cover trends, automation opportunities, user satisfaction, security, capacity and improvements.

Provider shall maintain a continual-improvement register and deliver at least two quantified improvement proposals per quarter. Client is not obliged to approve an investment.

## 14. Subcontractors and third parties

Provider may use disclosed subcontractors for after-hours language support or specialist repair but remains fully responsible. No subcontractor may access Client systems without equivalent written security and confidentiality obligations.

Provider shall coordinate with Client-selected hardware manufacturers, carriers and application teams but is not responsible for their delay. It shall continue escalation and document third-party dependencies.

## 15. Confidentiality and intellectual property

Each party shall protect confidential information for five years; security configurations, credentials and trade secrets remain protected while sensitive. Client owns its data, ticket content and Client-specific operating procedures. Provider owns pre-existing tools and general methods, granting Client a perpetual licence to use project-specific documentation paid for under this Agreement.

## 16. Audit and compliance

Provider shall maintain service, access, training, screening and security records for seven years. Client may audit annually on ten business days’ notice and after a material incident. Provider shall remediate material findings at its cost under an agreed plan.

Provider shall comply with applicable labour, tax, anti-bribery and data-protection laws and Client’s notified site policies.

## 17. Business continuity

Provider shall maintain geographically separated remote-delivery capability, quarterly call-tree tests and an annual continuity exercise. Critical service-desk intake shall resume within two hours after a Provider-site disruption. Essential ticket data shall have a recovery point objective of four hours.

## 18. Liability

Neither party is liable for indirect, special or consequential damages. Provider’s aggregate liability is capped at 150% of fees paid or payable in the preceding 12 months. The cap does not apply to fraud, wilful misconduct, confidentiality or data-security breach, IP infringement, employment claims involving Provider personnel, or death and personal injury.

Provider shall maintain professional indemnity and cyber insurance of at least INR 150,000,000 each.

## 19. Termination and exit

The initial term is three years. It renews automatically for one-year periods unless either party gives 120 days’ notice. Either party may terminate an uncured material breach after 30 days. Client may terminate immediately for serious security breach, corruption, insolvency or repeated chronic service failure.

Client may terminate for convenience after the first 18 months on 90 days’ notice, paying an exit charge of two months’ base fee if termination occurs before month 30.

Provider shall provide up to 90 days of exit assistance, transfer ticket history, asset records, procedures and open problems, support knowledge transfer, and revoke access at Client-directed times. Exit services within normal staffing are included; incremental resources require approved rates.

## 20. Dispute resolution

Disputes shall be escalated to service executives for 15 business days and then finally resolved by a sole arbitrator seated in Hyderabad, in English, under Indian law. Courts in Hyderabad may grant interim relief.
