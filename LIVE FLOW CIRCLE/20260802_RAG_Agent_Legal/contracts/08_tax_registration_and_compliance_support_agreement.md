# India Tax Registration and Ongoing Compliance Support Agreement

> **Synthetic demo document.** All parties, tax positions, identifiers, amounts and facts are fictional and created only for contract-search and RAG testing. This document is not tax or legal advice and must not be executed.

This Agreement is entered into on 15 February 2026 by **Oriole Tax & Accounting Advisory LLP**, Hyderabad (“Tax Provider”), and **Kepler Clinical Data India Private Limited**, Hyderabad (“Client”), a fictitious subsidiary of a United Kingdom clinical-software company.

## 1. Purpose

The Client is establishing a clinical-data and analytics GCC in Hyderabad. The Tax Provider will coordinate initial registrations and routine tax compliance based on records, positions and approvals supplied by the Client. The parties acknowledge that tax authorities retain discretion and that the Client remains the taxpayer responsible for returns and payments.

## 2. Registration services

The Tax Provider shall assist with:

- Goods and Services Tax registration in Telangana;
- tax deduction and collection account activation and portal access;
- professional tax employer and enrolment registrations;
- income-tax e-filing account setup;
- authorised-signatory digital access; and
- a registration memorandum listing credentials, filing frequencies and known due dates.

The fixed fee includes one application and one response to routine clarification for each registration. A rejected application requiring material rework is additional work if rejection results from changed or inaccurate Client information.

## 3. Ongoing compliance services

During the Term, the Tax Provider will:

1. prepare monthly or quarterly GST returns applicable to the Client;
2. reconcile sales, purchase and input-tax-credit data and report material exceptions;
3. calculate withholding-tax liabilities from Client-coded ledgers and prepare related returns;
4. prepare quarterly advance-tax estimates and an annual corporate income-tax return;
5. coordinate one annual tax audit with the Client’s appointed auditor;
6. prepare standard employee withholding reconciliations based on payroll outputs;
7. maintain a tax compliance calendar and monthly status report;
8. review intercompany charge summaries for consistency with the Client-approved cost-plus policy; and
9. coordinate data collection for a separate transfer-pricing accountant.

“Material exception” means a reconciliation difference exceeding INR 100,000 for one item or INR 500,000 in aggregate for a filing period.

## 4. Transfer pricing boundary

The engagement includes data coordination and a reasonableness check against the documented intercompany markup. It does not include an economic benchmarking study, accountant’s report, master file, country-by-country report, advance pricing agreement, mutual agreement procedure or defence of a transfer-pricing adjustment. Those services require a separate engagement.

The Tax Provider may flag an apparent inconsistency but will not alter the Client’s intercompany pricing without written approval.

## 5. Calendar and Client cut-offs

The Client shall provide:

- final monthly trial balance and GST ledgers by the fourth business day after month-end;
- withholding data and vendor tax documentation by the fifth business day;
- payroll reports by the third business day;
- quarterly forecasts at least fifteen days before an advance-tax due date; and
- board-approved annual accounts at least thirty days before the income-tax return due date.

The Tax Provider will provide draft routine returns at least three business days before the applicable due date if complete data is received on time. The Client shall approve or comment within one business day. No filing will be submitted without authorised approval, except where a standing written authority expressly permits submission.

## 6. Client obligations

The Client is responsible for accurate books, lawful invoices, tax positions, employee classifications, permanent-establishment analysis and timely payment of tax. The Client shall preserve supporting documents, grant system access on least-privilege terms, and disclose related-party transactions and authority notices.

The Client will designate its India Finance Controller as approval owner. Silence is not approval. If the Client misses a cut-off, the Tax Provider will identify likely consequences and use reasonable efforts to meet the deadline, but is not responsible for resulting interest or penalties.

## 7. Fees

The total fee for the twelve-month Term is **INR 2,380,000**, exclusive of taxes:

- INR 400,000 for registrations, payable 50% at commencement and 50% upon delivery of the registration memorandum;
- INR 1,680,000 for ongoing compliance, invoiced monthly at INR 140,000; and
- INR 300,000 for annual income-tax return and audit coordination, invoiced on delivery of the first draft return.

Government charges, specialist opinions, travel and third-party certificates are additional at cost. Work responding to tax notices is billed at agreed hourly rates after a written estimate. Invoices are due within twenty-one days.

## 8. Tax notices and audits

The Client shall send any tax notice to the Tax Provider within two business days of receipt. The Tax Provider will acknowledge it within one business day, record the response deadline and propose a scope. Routine portal clarifications concerning a return prepared under this Agreement are included up to four professional hours per notice. Representation, hearings, appeals, litigation and extensive document reconstruction are excluded.

## 9. Standard of care and reliance

The Tax Provider will exercise the care reasonably expected of a qualified Indian tax adviser. Advice is based on law and published guidance available when delivered and the facts disclosed by the Client. The Tax Provider is not obliged to update completed advice for later legal changes unless engaged to do so.

No tax result, refund, credit availability or authority response is guaranteed. Oral comments are preliminary unless confirmed in writing.

## 10. Confidentiality, security and tax data

Tax returns, payroll records, intercompany data and credentials are Highly Confidential. Access will be restricted to assigned personnel and approved subcontractors. Credentials must be stored in an encrypted vault with multi-factor authentication. Tax working papers may not be used to train public generative-AI models.

The Tax Provider will notify the Client within twenty-four hours after confirming unauthorised access to tax credentials or personal data and will cooperate with containment. Engagement records will be retained for eight years after the relevant assessment year, then securely destroyed unless a hold applies.

## 11. Subcontractors

The Tax Provider may use its disclosed affiliate, Oriole Compliance Operations Private Limited, for data preparation in India. It remains responsible for that affiliate’s work. Any offshore processing or new material subcontractor requires prior written consent.

## 12. Compliance

Both parties shall comply with anti-bribery, sanctions, tax-evasion facilitation and data-protection laws. The Tax Provider shall not submit information it knows to be false. If unresolved concerns arise, it may decline a filing, suspend affected work or make a disclosure required by professional rules.

## 13. Liability

Neither party is liable for indirect, consequential, special or punitive damages. Subject to law, the Tax Provider’s aggregate liability is limited to 150% of fees paid under this Agreement. For a penalty directly caused solely by the Tax Provider’s negligent failure to file a complete and approved return by the deadline, the Tax Provider will reimburse the penalty and related statutory interest, within the aggregate cap.

The cap does not apply to fraud, wilful misconduct or deliberate breach of confidentiality. The Tax Provider is not liable for tax itself, loss of a tax benefit based on a disputed position, or consequences of inaccurate or late Client data.

## 14. Termination

The Term runs through 14 February 2027. Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within fifteen days. The Client may terminate for convenience on thirty days’ notice. Upon termination, the Tax Provider will identify pending filings and deliver completed workpapers after payment of undisputed fees. The Client is responsible for appointing a successor and authorising credential changes.
