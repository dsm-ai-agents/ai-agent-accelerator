# Pune GCC Establishment Program Management Agreement

> **Synthetic demo document.** The parties and terms are wholly fictitious and are provided only as sample data for RAG and contract-indexing exercises.

This Agreement is effective 1 March 2026 between **Cedar Arc India Advisory Private Limited** (**PMO Provider**) and **Eisenmere Motion Systems GmbH** (**Client**), a German automotive engineering company establishing a Pune GCC.

## 1. Engagement

The Client appoints the PMO Provider to coordinate the establishment of a 175-person engineering and digital operations center in Pune. The PMO Provider will provide governance, integrated planning, risk and dependency management, status reporting, vendor coordination, financial tracking, decision support, and launch-readiness assurance.

The PMO Provider does not replace the Client’s management, licensed advisers, construction manager, recruiters, technology integrators, or statutory officers. It may coordinate those parties but is not responsible for their professional conclusions or performance.

## 2. Program outcomes

The target soft launch is 2 November 2026, with 75 operational personnel. The target full launch is 1 February 2027, with at least 125 personnel and core finance, HR, IT, security, workplace, and compliance processes operational.

Program outcomes are planning objectives rather than warranties. Achievement depends on Client decisions, regulatory processing, vendor performance, recruitment conditions, and timely funding.

## 3. Workstreams

The integrated program covers:

1. entity incorporation, banking, registrations, and corporate governance;
2. tax, transfer-pricing, accounting, and treasury readiness;
3. office selection, lease, design, fit-out, health and safety;
4. network, end-user computing, identity, cybersecurity, and service desk;
5. organization design, recruiting, employment, payroll, benefits, and policies;
6. procurement, vendor onboarding, insurance, and contract administration;
7. communications, leadership onboarding, stakeholder engagement, and launch; and
8. operational controls, business continuity, and post-launch stabilization.

## 4. PMO services

### 4.1 Integrated plan

The PMO Provider will maintain a dependency-linked plan identifying activities, owners, dates, decision gates, milestones, and critical path. Workstream owners remain accountable for execution and accuracy of their workstream plans.

### 4.2 Registers

The PMO Provider will maintain:

- a risk, assumption, issue, and dependency register;
- a decision log;
- an action tracker;
- a change register;
- a budget and commitment tracker based on supplied data;
- a vendor and contract register;
- a launch-readiness checklist; and
- a benefits and outcomes tracker.

The PMO Provider owns administration of these registers. The Client owns business decisions and risk acceptance.

### 4.3 Meetings and reporting

Workstream reviews occur weekly. The core program team meets each Tuesday. The Steering Committee meets on the second Thursday of each month. A concise weekly report will show milestone status, top risks, decisions required, budget position, headcount progress, and actions.

Minutes and decisions will be circulated within two business days. If no correction is received within three business days, minutes are treated as an accurate record, but silence does not approve expenditure or contractual change.

### 4.4 Vendor coordination

The PMO Provider may facilitate tender schedules, bidder questions, evaluation consolidation, contracting dependencies, onboarding, and performance reporting. The Client selects vendors and signs contracts. The PMO Provider cannot commit Client funds or waive vendor obligations unless expressly authorized.

### 4.5 Launch readiness

At least forty-five days before each launch, the PMO Provider will conduct a readiness review covering people, premises, systems, controls, statutory items, vendor support, communications, incident response, and business continuity. Red items require a documented owner and resolution or formal risk acceptance by the Steering Committee.

## 5. Deliverables

| Deliverable | Frequency or due date |

| Program charter and governance map | 15 March 2026 |
| Integrated baseline plan | 31 March 2026 |
| Weekly status report | Each Friday |
| Steering pack | Two business days before monthly meeting |
| Budget and commitment report | Monthly |
| Soft-launch readiness report | 18 September 2026 |
| Full-launch readiness report | 15 December 2026 |
| Stabilization and closure report | 28 February 2027 |

Deliverables will be in English using the Client’s approved templates where practical.

## 6. Staffing

The PMO Provider will assign one India Program Director at approximately 80% capacity, two Program Managers full-time, one analyst full-time, and subject-matter advisers for up to forty person-days across the term.

Named personnel may be replaced with comparably qualified personnel after reasonable notice. The Client may request replacement for documented performance, conduct, security, or conflict concerns. Replacement will not unreasonably disrupt the program.

## 7. Client responsibilities

The Client will appoint an executive sponsor and empowered workstream owners; make decisions within agreed deadlines; provide accurate plans, budgets, contracts, and status information; ensure vendor participation; approve expenditures; and provide access to relevant personnel and systems.

The Client will provide consolidated comments on deliverables. Conflicting stakeholder instructions will be escalated to the Client Program Sponsor, whose written decision governs.

## 8. Governance and decision rights

The Steering Committee is chaired by the Client’s Executive Sponsor and includes the India GCC Sponsor, Finance Lead, Technology Lead, HR Lead, and PMO Program Director.

Only the Client may approve business case changes, vendor awards, hiring plan, location, lease, capital expenditure, security exceptions, go-live, or risk acceptance above the Client’s tolerance. The PMO Provider may recommend and document but not decide these matters.

Urgent decisions required to protect safety or prevent material loss may be escalated by telephone and recorded afterward. The PMO Provider has no emergency authority to spend Client funds.

## 9. Fees and expenses

The fixed fee is **EUR 1,320,000**, exclusive of taxes, invoiced monthly in twelve equal installments of EUR 110,000. Up to forty adviser days and ordinary local travel in Pune are included.

Pre-approved international travel, travel outside Pune, specialist days beyond the included allowance, translation, data subscriptions, and project software requested by the Client are reimbursable. Additional adviser days are EUR 1,450 per day.

Invoices are payable within thirty days. The Client must notify a good-faith dispute within ten business days and pay undisputed amounts.

## 10. Baseline and change control

The initial baseline will be approved by 31 March 2026. A proposed change to launch dates, headcount, location, major scope, workstream count, reporting obligations, or staffing must be recorded in a change request.

The PMO Provider will assess schedule, cost, resource, and risk effects within five business days. A change is effective only when the Client Sponsor and PMO Provider’s authorized representative approve it. Emergency work may proceed on email authorization with a not-to-exceed amount, followed by formal documentation.

## 11. Dependencies and delay

The PMO Provider will flag an at-risk dependency promptly. If a Client or third-party dependency is late, the Parties will re-sequence work where reasonable. A delay caused by the Client or its vendors extends affected dates and may require additional fees where the PMO Provider cannot reasonably redeploy committed resources.

The PMO Provider will not be liable for delay caused by government processing, landlord handover, utility activation, vendor breach, Client decision delay, hiring market conditions, or force majeure, except to the extent it failed to identify or manage a known dependency with reasonable professional care.

## 12. Quality and acceptance

The PMO Provider will perform with reasonable skill and care. The Client will review milestone deliverables within five business days and identify material gaps against agreed requirements. The PMO Provider will correct validated gaps within a reasonable time without additional fee.

Reports are based on information received. The PMO Provider is not responsible for an inaccuracy caused by data it could not reasonably detect as incomplete or incorrect.

## 13. Confidentiality

Each Party will protect the other’s non-public business, technical, financial, personnel, and program information. Access will be limited to persons with a need to know and comparable duties. Confidentiality obligations continue for five years, and longer for trade secrets and personal data as required by law.

Disclosure required by law is allowed after notice where lawful. Information already lawfully known, independently developed, lawfully received, or public without breach is excluded.

## 14. Security and data

The PMO Provider will use Client-approved collaboration systems where provided, multifactor authentication, managed devices, and role-based access. Program records will not be stored in personal accounts.

A confirmed security incident affecting Client data will be reported within thirty-six hours. At completion, the PMO Provider will return or delete Client data, except one secure archival copy retained for legal compliance.

The Parties do not expect the PMO Provider to process production customer or vehicle data. Any such processing requires a separate written security and data-processing arrangement.

## 15. Intellectual property

Client owns final Client-specific program plans, registers, reports, and governance materials upon payment. The PMO Provider retains its templates, methods, tools, accelerators, and know-how. It grants the Client a perpetual licence to embedded Provider materials necessary for internal use of the deliverables.

The Client grants the PMO Provider a limited licence to Client materials solely to perform the Services.

## 16. Independence and conflicts

The PMO Provider is an independent contractor. It may serve other clients, including automotive businesses, if confidentiality and conflict safeguards are maintained.

The PMO Provider will disclose any financial interest, referral arrangement, or material relationship with a proposed vendor. It will not accept a success fee from a vendor selected under the program without the Client’s prior written consent.

## 17. Compliance

Both Parties will comply with applicable anti-corruption, sanctions, export-control, competition, employment, and data-protection laws. The PMO Provider will follow reasonable site safety and security requirements notified by the Client.

## 18. Liability and indemnity

The PMO Provider indemnifies the Client against third-party claims that a deliverable created solely by the PMO Provider infringes copyright, subject to standard exclusions for Client materials, modifications, and unauthorized combinations. The Client indemnifies the PMO Provider against claims arising from Client materials or unlawful Client instructions.

Neither Party is liable for indirect, consequential, punitive, or special damages, or loss of profit, production, revenue, opportunity, or goodwill. Aggregate liability is capped at the fees paid or payable in the preceding twelve months.

The cap does not apply to fraud, wilful misconduct, confidentiality breach, intellectual-property infringement, death or personal injury caused by negligence, or liability that cannot be limited. Confidentiality and security liability is capped at twice the total contract fee.

## 19. Termination

Either Party may terminate material breach not cured within twenty days after notice, or immediately for insolvency, bribery, or serious unauthorized disclosure.

The Client may terminate for convenience on thirty days’ notice. It will pay fees through termination, approved expenses, non-cancellable commitments, and a demobilization fee equal to one month’s fee if termination occurs before 31 August 2026.

On termination, the PMO Provider will deliver current plans and registers, identify open risks and decisions, and provide up to ten transition days included in fees already paid. Additional transition support is charged at agreed rates.

## 20. Dispute resolution

This Agreement is governed by Maharashtra law and applicable Indian law. Executives will attempt resolution within fifteen business days. Any unresolved dispute will be finally resolved by a sole arbitrator seated in Pune under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. Proceedings are in English. Courts in Pune have jurisdiction for interim relief.

## 21. General

Neither Party may assign without consent, except to an affiliate or successor that assumes the obligations. Subcontracting does not relieve the PMO Provider of responsibility.

Notices will be sent to the addresses in the signature block and designated legal email addresses. This Agreement and signed change orders are the entire agreement. Amendments and waivers must be in writing. Invalid provisions will be narrowed while the remainder remains effective. Counterparts and electronic signatures are valid.
