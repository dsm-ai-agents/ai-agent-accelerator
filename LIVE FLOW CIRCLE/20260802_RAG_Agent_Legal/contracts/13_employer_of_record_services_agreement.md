# India Interim Employer of Record Services Agreement

> **Synthetic demo document.** The organizations, individuals and commercial facts below are entirely fictitious. This agreement is for RAG evaluation only and is not legal, tax or employment advice.

This Agreement is entered into on 10 January 2026 between Fjordlight Robotics AS of Norway (“Client”) and SaffronOrbit Employment Services Private Limited of Pune (“EOR”). Meridian Harbor GCC Advisory Private Limited (“GCC Adviser”) coordinates establishment of the Client’s planned Indian subsidiary but is not a party to employment relationships.

## 1. Purpose and duration

Pending incorporation and operational readiness of Fjordlight Robotics India Private Limited (“Indian Entity”), the EOR will employ up to 28 India-based personnel selected by the Client and assign them to perform permissible services for the Client. The initial period ends on 9 October 2026. It may continue month-to-month for up to three months at a 10% administrative-fee premium.

## 2. EOR services

The EOR will issue locally compliant employment agreements; enroll employees in payroll and benefits; withhold and remit applicable taxes and statutory contributions; administer leave and expenses; maintain employment records; provide standard HR support; and, when directed lawfully, manage discipline or separation.

The EOR is the legal employer. The Client controls business objectives, technical work, project priorities and performance feedback. Nothing authorizes the Client to sign employment documents in the EOR’s name or directly terminate an employee.

## 3. Hiring and employment terms

The Client shall provide an approved candidate, role, fixed compensation, variable-pay rules, work location and proposed start date. The EOR shall review the request for legal and payroll feasibility and normally issue a contract within five Business Days after receiving complete information.

Any probation, notice, leave, bonus, intellectual-property, restrictive-covenant and remote-work terms must comply with law and EOR policy. The Client may propose enhanced benefits, but deviations must be documented and funded. Employees will receive no equity unless governed by a separately approved plan.

## 4. Fees and pre-funding

The Client shall pay:

- a one-time onboarding fee of INR 35,000 per employee;
- a monthly administration fee of INR 42,000 per active employee;
- actual gross compensation, employer contributions, insurance premiums, reimbursable expenses and taxes; and
- separation costs, notice pay, gratuity, accrued leave and legally required amounts.

At least seven Business Days before each payroll date, the Client shall pre-fund the complete payroll estimate and fees. The EOR need not advance Client payroll. If timely funding is not received, the EOR may use a Client security deposit, suspend new hiring and charge 1% monthly interest, but shall take reasonable steps to protect employee wage rights.

The Client shall maintain a security deposit equal to two months of forecast employment cost, initially INR 4,200,000. The EOR will reconcile the deposit within 60 days after all employment liabilities are settled.

## 5. Payroll and benefits

Payroll is processed on the last Business Day of each month. Variable inputs and approved expenses are due by the tenth calendar day. Late items roll to the next payroll unless law requires correction sooner. The Client approves the payroll preview within two Business Days; silence constitutes approval only for unchanged fixed items.

Standard benefits include group medical insurance for employee, spouse and two children; group term life; accident cover; statutory leave; and provident fund as applicable. Client-specific benefits require written amendment.

## 6. Client direction and workplace

The Client shall provide meaningful duties, trained managers, secure systems, a safe workplace and working-time instructions consistent with law. It shall not direct unlawful discrimination, misclassify compensation, promise employment terms, request off-payroll payments or expose employees to controlled technology without authorization.

The EOR may reject instructions presenting material legal, safety, sanctions, tax or ethical risk. The parties will seek a lawful alternative. The EOR is not responsible for product quality or technical deliverables.

## 7. Permanent-establishment and tax disclaimer

The EOR provides employment administration only. It does not warrant that the arrangement avoids a permanent establishment, taxable presence, transfer-pricing obligation, corporate registration or sector licence for the Client. The Client shall obtain its own tax and regulatory advice and shall not authorize employees to conclude contracts, hold inventory, collect revenue or represent a permanent office unless separately approved.

## 8. Employee conduct and performance

The Client shall document concerns and provide relevant evidence. The EOR will determine an appropriate lawful process after consulting the Client. Serious security or safety risks may be removed from Client access immediately. Employment action remains the EOR’s decision, though the Client bears resulting costs unless caused by EOR breach.

Employees must comply with both EOR employment policies and Client workplace/security policies. Where policies conflict, law and the employment agreement prevail.

## 9. Termination of employment

The Client may request separation with written reasons and funding. The EOR shall assess notice, hearing, documentation and statutory requirements. The Client pays salary through termination, notice or pay in lieu, accrued benefits, severance required by law, legal expenses approved in advance and any settlement authorized by the Client.

The EOR indemnifies the Client only to the extent additional separation cost results directly from the EOR’s failure to follow the agreed lawful process. The EOR may terminate for employee misconduct or legal necessity after consultation where practicable.

## 10. Migration to Indian Entity

Once the Indian Entity has bank, payroll, registrations, insurance and compliant employment documentation, the parties will agree a migration date. The intended approach is consensual resignation and rehire with continuous-service recognition where feasible; no automatic transfer is promised.

At least 45 days before migration, the Client shall provide readiness evidence. The EOR will supply employee data, balances and payroll records with consent; issue transition communications; reconcile benefits; and support offers. The migration fee is INR 20,000 per employee. The Client and Indian Entity shall fund all accrued obligations and honor agreed continuity terms. Employees remain free to decline.

## 11. Data protection and confidentiality

The EOR may process identity, bank, tax, compensation, benefits, attendance and dependent data for employment administration. It shall limit access, encrypt transfers, maintain logs, use approved processors and notify the Client within 24 hours of a confirmed breach affecting Client-associated employees.

Each party shall protect confidential information for five years, and personal information for as long as law requires. Employment records will be retained for statutory periods and securely deleted thereafter. Cross-border access by the Client requires appropriate notices, controls and lawful transfer arrangements.

## 12. Intellectual property

Employment agreements shall assign to the Client or its nominated affiliate, to the extent lawful, intellectual property created in the course of Client-directed work, subject to employee moral rights and statutory rights. The Client grants the EOR limited rights to administer such provisions. The EOR obtains no commercial right in Client work product.

## 13. Compliance

The EOR shall maintain employment registrations, payroll records and statutory remittances applicable to it. The Client remains responsible for export controls, industry licences, product regulation, technical supervision and its corporate taxes. Both parties shall comply with anti-bribery, sanctions and anti-harassment laws.

The Client may audit relevant payroll and remittance records annually on ten Business Days’ notice, subject to employee privacy and redaction of unrelated information.

## 14. Indemnities and liability

The EOR indemnifies the Client against claims caused by EOR failure to pay funded wages or remit funded statutory amounts, or by EOR breach of confidentiality, data duties or employment obligations. The Client indemnifies the EOR against claims arising from Client work instructions, workplace conditions, product activities, discrimination by Client managers, failure to fund, tax presence or Client-requested termination.

Neither party is liable for indirect loss. General liability is capped at six months of administration fees. Liability for data breach, confidentiality breach or indemnified employment claims is capped at the greater of INR 20,000,000 and 12 months of total administration fees. Fraud, wilful misconduct and payment obligations are uncapped.

## 15. Termination of Agreement

Either party may terminate for uncured material breach after 30 days, insolvency, sanctions exposure, bribery or material non-payment. The Client may terminate on 60 days’ notice. Ending this Agreement does not itself end employment. The Client must fund continued employment through lawful transfer or separation.

## 16. Disputes and general

The EOR is an independent contractor. The GCC Adviser is not a guarantor, co-employer or payroll funder. Amendments require signature. Neither party may publicize the relationship without consent.

Senior representatives will negotiate disputes for 20 Business Days. Remaining disputes shall be finally resolved by one arbitrator seated in Pune, in English, under Indian arbitration law. Maharashtra law governs.
