# Intercompany GCC Services and Cost-Plus Implementation Agreement

> **Synthetic demo document.** Every entity, person, address, figure and transaction in this agreement is fictional and supplied only for RAG and contract-analytics demonstrations. It is not legal or tax advice and must not be relied upon.

This Agreement is entered into on 1 April 2026 between **LumenArc Systems India Private Limited**, a fictitious Indian company operating at 88 Horizon Plaza, Gurugram 122002 (“India Service Company”), and **LumenArc Systems Pte. Ltd.**, a fictitious Singapore company at 20 Meridian Quay, Singapore 018987 (“Principal”). Each is a “Party” and together the “Parties.”

## 1. Background

The Principal develops enterprise logistics software and owns the group’s core platform, trademarks, customer relationships and product roadmap. The India Service Company operates a captive GCC providing routine development support, quality assurance, finance operations and internal analytics. The Parties wish to document the services, cost allocation, cost-plus remuneration, intellectual-property boundaries and governance applicable to their relationship.

## 2. Services

The India Service Company shall provide:

1. software development support against specifications and priorities approved by the Principal;
2. quality assurance, test automation and release-validation support;
3. internal data reporting and operational analytics;
4. accounts-payable processing, ledger support and management-report preparation;
5. procurement administration for approved group vendors;
6. internal service-desk and application support; and
7. related incidental services agreed in an annual service plan.

The India Service Company will not contract with external customers, determine group product strategy, set customer pricing, own customer receivables, enter treasury transactions, or assume market, credit or product-liability risk unless authorised in a signed amendment.

## 3. Annual service plan

Before each financial year, the Parties will approve a plan stating functions, forecast headcount, senior roles, budget, capital requirements, delivery metrics and material subcontractors. A plan does not amend the pricing method unless it expressly says so.

The initial plan assumes 140 full-time equivalent personnel: 78 engineering and quality personnel, 22 analytics personnel, 26 finance and procurement personnel, and 14 management and shared-support personnel.

## 4. Principal responsibilities

The Principal shall:

- provide specifications, priorities, policies, systems access and timely decisions;
- retain control over product roadmap, customer contracting and strategic risk;
- fund approved operating costs and working-capital needs;
- maintain rights to any tools or data supplied to the India Service Company;
- review deliverables and notify material defects; and
- provide information required for invoicing, foreign-exchange and transfer-pricing compliance.

The India Service Company is not liable for delay caused by contradictory or late instructions.

## 5. Cost base

“Operating Cost Base” means direct and indirect costs reasonably incurred by the India Service Company in providing the Services, consistently determined under Indian accounting standards and the group allocation policy. Included costs are:

- employee compensation and benefits;
- recruitment and training;
- office rent, facilities and utilities;
- technology, software and communications;
- depreciation of GCC assets;
- approved professional services;
- travel related to the Services; and
- an allocated share of local management and administration.

The Cost Base excludes corporate income tax, interest, foreign-exchange gains or losses, penalties, shareholder expenses, dividends, costs of a material breach, costs unrelated to Services, exceptional restructuring not approved by the Principal, and pass-through costs expressly designated as no-markup items.

## 6. Remuneration and markup

The Principal shall pay the India Service Company the Operating Cost Base plus a **12.5% markup**. The Parties selected this markup based on a fictitious benchmarking memorandum dated 15 March 2026. Pass-through software licences purchased solely as agent for the Principal and government filing fees will be reimbursed without markup where separately identified.

The forecast value over the initial three-year term is **USD 12,600,000**, but actual consideration depends on incurred costs. This forecast is not a minimum purchase commitment.

## 7. Allocation methodology

Direct costs will be assigned to the function benefiting from them. Shared people costs will be allocated using time records or headcount. Premises costs will be allocated by occupied seats; technology costs by assigned users or consumption; and general administration by headcount unless another driver more reliably reflects benefit.

The India Service Company shall document material allocation keys and apply them consistently. Any change expected to alter annual charges by more than 3% requires written approval from both finance leads.

## 8. Invoicing and true-up

The India Service Company will issue monthly invoices in US dollars within ten business days after month-end, based on actual booked costs plus markup. Supporting schedules will show cost category, excluded costs, pass-through items, allocation adjustments, markup and applicable indirect taxes.

Invoices are payable within thirty days to the designated Indian bank account, subject to withholding required by law. The Principal will provide withholding certificates. Bank fees charged by the remitting bank are borne by the Principal.

Quarterly estimated-to-actual reconciliations will be performed. A final annual true-up will be invoiced or credited within ninety days after financial year-end. Errors above USD 10,000 will be corrected promptly; smaller items may be included in the next true-up.

## 9. Taxes and foreign exchange

Amounts are exclusive of applicable indirect taxes. Each Party is responsible for its own income taxes and statutory filings. The Parties will cooperate on tax residency certificates, remittance forms, withholding evidence, GST export documentation and foreign-exchange reporting.

Nothing in this Agreement guarantees a tax treatment, export-of-services status, deduction or absence of permanent establishment. Each Party will obtain independent tax advice.

## 10. Transfer-pricing documentation and adjustment

Each Party will maintain records reasonably needed to support the arm’s-length character of the arrangement. The India Service Company will prepare its local file and accountant’s report as required under Indian law. The Principal will provide group information needed for master-file or related disclosures.

If a final authority determination or updated benchmarking establishes that the markup is outside an arm’s-length range, the Parties will discuss a prospective or compensating adjustment consistent with law. Neither Party is required to accept double taxation without pursuing reasonably available relief. Any adjustment must be documented by invoice, credit note or other lawful record.

## 11. Personnel and control

India Service Company personnel remain its employees and under its day-to-day supervision. The Principal may set desired outcomes, information-security policies and delivery priorities but will not directly hire, discipline or terminate India personnel. The India Service Company is responsible for payroll, benefits, workplace obligations and employment compliance.

Key-role appointments require consultation with the Principal, but final employment decisions remain with the India Service Company.

## 12. Intellectual property

The Principal retains all rights in pre-existing group technology, customer information, specifications, brands and materials (“Principal Materials”). It grants the India Service Company a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence during the Term solely to perform Services.

To the extent permitted by law, all deliverables, source code, test assets, documentation, analyses and inventions specifically created in performing Services (“Developed IP”) vest in the Principal upon creation. Where immediate vesting is ineffective, the India Service Company assigns the Developed IP to the Principal and will obtain compatible employee assignments.

The India Service Company retains pre-existing administrative tools and general skills. If its pre-existing material is embedded in a deliverable, it grants the Principal a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free licence to use that material as part of the deliverable.

## 13. Data security and confidentiality

Each Party will protect the other’s confidential information and use it only for this Agreement. The India Service Company will apply the Principal’s approved security baseline, including role-based access, multi-factor authentication, endpoint management, logging and annual security training.

A confirmed security incident affecting Principal data must be reported within twelve hours, followed by daily material updates until containment. Personal data will be processed only on documented instructions and in accordance with any separate data-processing addendum.

Confidentiality continues for five years after termination; trade secrets remain protected while legally qualifying as trade secrets.

## 14. Governance, records and audit

Operational leads will meet monthly and finance leads quarterly. An executive committee will approve the annual plan, material scope changes and unresolved service issues.

The India Service Company will retain invoices, allocation workings, time data and cost-base records for eight years. The Principal may audit relevant records once each year on fifteen business days’ notice through an independent adviser bound by confidentiality. Audits may not access unrelated employee personal data or third-party confidential information. If an audit finds overbilling above 2% for the audited year, the India Service Company will reimburse reasonable audit costs and issue a credit.

## 15. Warranties and compliance

Each Party warrants it is duly authorised and will comply with applicable anti-bribery, sanctions, export-control, tax and data-protection laws. The India Service Company warrants that Services will be performed with reasonable skill and care.

Except as expressly stated, no implied warranties apply. The India Service Company does not warrant commercial success, error-free software or achievement of the Principal’s business forecasts.

## 16. Indemnities and liability

The India Service Company indemnifies the Principal against third-party claims arising from its employment obligations, wilful infringement by Developed IP, or wilful misconduct. The Principal indemnifies the India Service Company against claims arising from Principal Materials, external customer commitments, or instructions that violate law.

Neither Party is liable for indirect, consequential or punitive loss, loss of goodwill or lost external revenue. Aggregate liability is capped at the charges paid or payable in the twelve months preceding the claim. The cap does not apply to fraud, wilful misconduct, confidentiality breach, infringement indemnities or unpaid charges.

## 17. Term and termination

The initial Term runs from 1 April 2026 through 31 March 2029 and then renews annually unless either Party gives 120 days’ notice. Either Party may terminate for material breach not cured within thirty days, insolvency, or illegality.

On termination, the India Service Company will cease using Principal Materials, return data, provide a final cost true-up and support an orderly transition for up to ninety days at the same pricing method. The Principal will pay undisputed accrued charges and approved wind-down costs, including unavoidable employee or lease costs that cannot reasonably be mitigated.

## 18. Dispute resolution

Finance disputes will first be reviewed by the two Chief Financial Officers. Other disputes will be reviewed by executive committee members. If unresolved after thirty days, the dispute will be finally resolved by arbitration under the SIAC Rules by one arbitrator, seated in Singapore, in English. Either Party may seek urgent injunctive relief from a competent court.

## 19. General

This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales, excluding conflict rules. Neither Party may assign it outside the LumenArc group without consent; an intragroup assignee must assume all obligations. Force majeure excuses affected delay but not accrued payment. No amendment is effective unless signed by authorised representatives. This Agreement, the annual plan and incorporated schedules are the entire agreement. Electronic signatures and counterparts are valid.
