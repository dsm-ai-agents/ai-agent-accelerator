# Finance and Accounting Managed Services Agreement

> **Synthetic demo document.** All organizations, individuals, addresses, figures, and circumstances in this file are fictitious. It is designed only for a contract-search and RAG demonstration and must not be treated as legal, tax, or accounting advice.

This Agreement is entered into on 1 January 2026 by **Caldera Mobility GmbH**, a fictitious German mobility-technology company (**Client**), and **BlueLedger GCC Operations Private Limited**, a fictitious Indian company at 44 Senapati Bapat Road, Pune 411016 (**Provider**).

The Client operates a Pune GCC through Caldera Mobility India Private Limited. It requires controlled transaction-accounting and reporting support while retaining all statutory and fiduciary decisions.

## 1. Term and scope

The term is 24 months from the Effective Date. The parties may extend it only through a written renewal signed at least 60 days before expiry.

The Provider shall deliver:

1. accounts-payable intake, duplicate checks, three-way-match support, exception routing, and payment-proposal preparation;
2. accounts-receivable cash application, aging reports, and collection-worklist preparation;
3. bank and balance-sheet reconciliations;
4. fixed-asset register maintenance and depreciation runs based on Client policy;
5. journal preparation and workflow submission;
6. month-end close coordination and management reporting;
7. treasury cash-position reports, without authority to move funds;
8. audit schedules and document coordination; and
9. process documentation, control evidence, and KPI reporting.

The Provider shall support the Indian entity and two designated European cost centers. Other entities require change control.

## 2. Express exclusions

The Provider shall not select accounting policies, sign financial statements, file tax returns, authorize a payment, open or operate bank accounts, approve credit, make provisioning judgments, provide a statutory audit, or represent the Client before an authority. Payroll processing, transfer-pricing opinions, valuation, and legal advice are excluded.

The Client’s Chief Financial Officer retains responsibility for books, controls, financial statements, filings, liquidity, and all approvals.

## 3. Transition

The first eight weeks are the Transition Period. The Provider shall complete process discovery, desktop procedures, volume baselines, access mapping, segregation-of-duties analysis, opening-item validation, and knowledge-transfer sign-off.

The Client shall provide historic ledgers, chart of accounts, accounting policies, vendor and customer masters, bank statement access, close calendar, sample reconciliations, approval matrices, and knowledgeable personnel.

No process enters steady state until both process owners sign its readiness certificate. If the Client delays dependencies, milestones move by the documented period of impact.

## 4. Operating controls

The Provider shall use Client-approved systems and workflows. No individual who prepares a vendor master change may prepare or approve a payment proposal for that vendor. Privileged access shall be reviewed quarterly. Shared user IDs are prohibited.

Journal entries require documentary support, preparer identity, account rationale, cost-center coding, and Client approval. The Provider may upload approved entries but may not finally post manual entries above EUR 25,000 without a second Client approval.

Vendor-bank-detail changes require independent callback verification using a previously validated contact. Suspected fraud shall be frozen and escalated within 30 minutes.

## 5. Service Levels

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Correctly processed invoices | At least 99.3% |
| Standard invoices entered | Within 2 Business Days |
| Bank reconciliations | By Business Day 3 |
| Balance-sheet reconciliations | By Business Day 6 |
| Draft close pack | By Business Day 7 |
| Final management pack | By Business Day 9 |
| Critical control exception | Escalated within 30 minutes |
| Audit request acknowledgement | Within 1 Business Day |

Accuracy excludes errors in Client source data that the Provider could not reasonably detect. Timeliness pauses while a required approval or clarification is outstanding.

A missed critical target produces a credit equal to 3% of the monthly fee for the affected process. Credits are capped at 12% per month. Four misses of one target in six months require a corrective-action plan and entitle the Client to terminate the affected process if the next two measurement periods fail.

## 6. Governance

Process leads meet twice weekly during transition and weekly thereafter. A monthly governance meeting reviews volumes, backlog, service performance, control exceptions, staffing, automation, and forecast demand. Quarterly reviews address finance-transformation opportunities and unresolved risks.

The Provider shall maintain issue, risk, control, and decision logs. A high-risk control deficiency must have an interim control within one Business Day and a permanent remediation plan within five Business Days.

## 7. Fees

The Client shall pay EUR 30,000 per month, totaling EUR 720,000 over the term, plus applicable taxes. The fee includes monthly volumes of 3,000 supplier invoices, 1,200 cash applications, 180 balance-sheet reconciliations, and 250 journal preparations.

Above-baseline volumes are charged at EUR 4.50 per invoice, EUR 3.00 per cash application, EUR 22 per reconciliation, and EUR 12 per journal. A volume band applies only after volume exceeds baseline by 10% in two consecutive months.

Invoices are payable within 30 days. Approved travel outside Pune is reimbursed at cost. The Provider bears ordinary software, training, and local management costs.

## 8. Personnel

The Provider shall maintain a service-delivery manager, qualified accounting lead, control specialist, and sufficient analysts. Personnel processing German-language documents must meet B2 business proficiency. Background screening is required for personnel with financial-system access.

The Client may reasonably require removal of personnel for misconduct, security risk, repeated material error, or conflict of interest. The Provider shall provide a qualified replacement within ten Business Days without transition fees.

## 9. Data protection and security

Financial records are Client Confidential Information. The Provider shall apply least-privilege access, multifactor authentication, managed endpoints, encryption, download restrictions, quarterly access certification, and audit logging.

Client Data shall be hosted in India or the European Economic Area unless the Client approves another location. The Provider shall notify a suspected compromise within two hours, support investigation, and deliver root-cause analysis within seven Business Days.

Personal data shall be processed only on documented instructions and retained only for the service or mandatory legal retention. On exit, data shall be returned in commonly readable form and deleted from active systems within 30 days.

## 10. Books, audit, and regulatory cooperation

The Provider shall retain workpapers and control evidence for eight years. The Client and its statutory auditor may inspect relevant records during business hours on reasonable notice. Following suspected fraud, regulatory inquiry, or material control failure, immediate targeted access may be required.

The Provider shall respond to ordinary audit requests within three Business Days. Specialist reports, extensive historic reconstruction, and testimony are chargeable under an approved change order.

## 11. Confidentiality

Each party shall protect non-public information received from the other and use it only under this Agreement. Permitted disclosure is limited to personnel, Affiliates, auditors, and advisers with a need to know and equivalent duties. Legally compelled disclosure requires advance notice where permitted.

Financial and personal information obligations survive for seven years; trade-secret obligations survive as long as protected by law.

## 12. Intellectual property

The Client owns its ledgers, data, accounting policies, reports, and Client-specific workpapers after payment. The Provider retains its generic templates, workflow methods, automation components, and know-how. The Client receives a perpetual license to Provider materials embedded in a paid deliverable for internal use.

The Provider shall not train a public or shared machine-learning model on Client Data. Use of generative AI on Client Data requires prior written approval, an enterprise-isolated environment, and documented human review.

## 13. Compliance

The Provider shall comply with applicable anti-bribery, privacy, employment, and books-and-records laws. It shall not offer anything of value to influence an auditor, bank employee, official, supplier, or Client employee. Conflicts must be disclosed immediately.

The Provider shall maintain a whistleblowing channel and annually train assigned personnel on fraud indicators and financial controls.

## 14. Warranties and indemnities

The Provider warrants that services will be performed with professional care by appropriately trained personnel and materially conform to approved procedures. It shall reperform defective services without additional charge.

The Provider indemnifies the Client against third-party claims arising from Provider fraud, infringement by Provider materials, or breach of employment obligations to Provider personnel. The Client indemnifies the Provider for claims arising from unlawful instructions, Client-selected accounting treatments, or inaccurate Client representations to authorities.

## 15. Liability

Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss. Aggregate liability is limited to fees paid or payable in the preceding 12 months. The cap is two times that amount for breach of confidentiality, data protection, fraud-control procedures, or IP indemnity. Liability is unlimited for fraud, wilful misconduct, or liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

Service credits do not prevent recovery for a distinct breach producing direct loss, but no amount may be recovered twice.

## 16. Business continuity

The Provider shall maintain alternate work arrangements and recover critical close activities within four hours. It shall test continuity annually and before each fiscal year-end close. If systems are unavailable, the Provider shall use Client-approved manual controls and reconcile entries after restoration.

## 17. Change control

Changes to entities, systems, accounting standards, volumes, languages, close dates, or deliverables require a written impact assessment. No additional charge applies without a signed change order, except emergency steps expressly authorized by the Client Finance Director.

## 18. Termination

Either party may terminate for uncured material breach after 30 days, or for insolvency. The Client may terminate an affected process immediately for deliberate control circumvention, unauthorized payment activity, or repeated material reporting error.

If the Client terminates for convenience on 90 days’ notice, it shall pay fees through the notice period and unavoidable approved commitments. There is no other termination fee.

## 19. Exit

The Provider shall give up to 90 days of exit assistance, including open-item lists, reconciliations, workpapers, procedures, calendars, access inventories, and structured knowledge transfer. Forty hours per process are included; additional hours are billed at EUR 65.

The Provider shall revoke access on the date directed by the Client and certify return or deletion of data.

## 20. Disputes and general terms

The parties shall escalate disputes to their CFO and Managing Director for 15 Business Days. Unresolved disputes shall be arbitrated by one arbitrator in Mumbai, in English, under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. Indian law governs.

Neither party may assign without consent, except to an Affiliate that assumes the obligations. The Provider is an independent contractor. Force majeure excuses affected performance but not payment, security duties, or reasonable continuity measures. This Agreement and signed schedules are the entire agreement and may be amended only in writing.
