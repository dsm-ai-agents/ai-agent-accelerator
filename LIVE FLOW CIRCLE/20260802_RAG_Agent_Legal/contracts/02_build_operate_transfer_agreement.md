# Hyderabad Global Capability Center Build-Operate-Transfer Agreement

> **Synthetic demo document.** This agreement and every name, address, amount, and event in it are fictitious and created solely for a contract-search demonstration.

This Build-Operate-Transfer Agreement (**Agreement**) is made on 15 January 2026 between **Blue Banyan GCC Ventures Private Limited**, Hyderabad (**Operator**), and **Fablebridge Payments plc**, London, United Kingdom (**Client**).

## 1. Purpose

The Client wishes to establish a captive capability center in Hyderabad for payment operations engineering, fraud analytics, platform reliability, and compliance technology. The Operator will build and operate the center during a defined incubation period and transfer the agreed personnel, assets, contracts, documentation, and operational responsibility to the Client’s Indian subsidiary.

The planned scale is 250 personnel: 60 by 30 September 2026, 150 by 30 June 2027, and 250 by 30 June 2028. Headcount figures are targets dependent on approved roles, compensation, timely interviews, market conditions, and the Client’s hiring decisions.

## 2. Definitions

**India Entity** means Fablebridge Capability Services India Private Limited, once incorporated and eligible to receive the Transferred Operations.

**Transfer Date** means 31 March 2029 or another date agreed under Section 9.

**Transferred Operations** means the dedicated personnel, assets, assignable contracts, operating records, and processes identified in the Transfer Register.

**Service Commencement Date** means 1 April 2026.

**Readiness Criteria** means the conditions in Section 9.2.

## 3. Build phase

From the effective date through 31 March 2026, the Operator will establish the program office; support entity, tax, and banking advisers; secure interim managed workspace for up to 100 seats; design recruiting channels; configure finance, payroll, HR, procurement, and security processes; and prepare a launch readiness plan.

The Operator will not provide reserved legal or tax advice. The Client will appoint licensed advisers and approve all incorporation filings, tax positions, banking forms, and regulated submissions.

The Operator will create an initial control framework addressing access, segregation of duties, incident escalation, vendor diligence, background screening, equipment custody, and financial approvals. Client security policies prevail where supplied in time and reasonably capable of implementation.

## 4. Operate phase

From Service Commencement until Transfer, the Operator will provide:

- recruitment coordination and employment administration;
- payroll inputs, benefits coordination, and employee helpdesk;
- workplace and facilities management;
- finance operations, bookkeeping support, and management reporting;
- procurement coordination and vendor administration;
- IT service-desk coordination and asset inventory;
- compliance calendar administration; and
- governance, KPI reporting, and transition preparation.

The Operator may employ personnel through itself or an approved affiliate during incubation. Such personnel remain employees of the relevant Operator entity until lawfully transferred. The Client controls functional priorities and work product but will route employment actions through the Operator’s authorized HR team.

## 5. Recruitment and workforce

The Client will approve job descriptions, levels, compensation bands, interview panels, and offers. The Operator will source candidates, schedule interviews, administer approved assessments, conduct reference and background-check coordination, and issue offers through the employing entity.

The monthly hiring forecast will be finalized at least forty-five days before the relevant month. If the Client delays interview feedback beyond three business days or alters a role after sourcing begins, affected hiring dates will be adjusted.

The Operator will comply with applicable employment laws and maintain employment records. It will not dismiss, materially discipline, or change compensation for dedicated personnel without Client consultation, except where urgent action is reasonably required for safety, misconduct, legal compliance, or prevention of material harm.

## 6. Service levels

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Standard role shortlist | 10 business days from approved requisition |
| Critical-role shortlist | 15 business days |
| Payroll input accuracy | 99.5% for Operator-controlled inputs |
| Priority 1 operational incident acknowledgement | 30 minutes |
| Monthly management report | By 7th business day |
| Employee helpdesk first response | 1 business day |

Failure to meet a measure in two consecutive months requires a corrective action plan. If three or more material service levels are missed in a quarter for reasons within Operator control, the Client receives a service credit equal to 3% of that quarter’s management fees. Total credits are capped at 8% of quarterly management fees and are the Client’s sole monetary remedy for service-level failure, without limiting termination rights for persistent material breach.

## 7. Governance

A Joint Steering Committee (**JSC**) comprising two representatives from each Party will meet monthly. It approves the annual operating plan, headcount envelope, material vendors, budget changes, risk acceptances, and transfer readiness.

An operating review will occur weekly during Build and biweekly thereafter. The Operator will maintain risk, issue, decision, change, vendor, asset, and transfer registers. Matters involving personal safety, suspected fraud, material security incidents, or regulatory inquiry will be escalated immediately.

## 8. Charges and funding

The estimated aggregate contract value is **GBP 4,850,000**, excluding taxes, pass-through employment costs, facilities, equipment, and approved vendors.

The Client will pay:

- a Build fee of GBP 420,000 in four monthly installments;
- an Operate management fee of GBP 92,000 per month from April 2026 through March 2027;
- GBP 110,000 per month from April 2027 through March 2028;
- GBP 125,000 per month from April 2028 until Transfer; and
- a Transfer fee of GBP 650,000, 40% at transfer planning, 40% on legal transfer, and 20% on final acceptance.

Dedicated employment costs and approved third-party costs are reimbursed at cost. The Operator may apply a 4% administration charge to third-party costs it contracts and pays, excluding statutory payments and employee payroll. The Client will pre-fund payroll and material pass-through costs ten business days before due date.

Invoices are payable within thirty days. The Client may dispute an invoice within fifteen days but must pay undisputed amounts. Foreign-exchange gains or losses on INR pass-through costs are for the Client’s account using the bank conversion rate actually incurred.

## 9. Transfer

### 9.1 Transfer planning

Formal transfer planning begins no later than 1 April 2028. The Parties will agree a detailed plan covering employees, assets, contracts, data, records, systems access, licenses, statutory registrations, communications, benefits continuity, and cutover.

### 9.2 Readiness Criteria

Transfer may occur when:

1. the India Entity is validly incorporated, banked, registered, and authorized to employ personnel;
2. it has approved payroll, benefits, finance, procurement, data-protection, and workplace processes;
3. at least 90% of in-scope process documents have been delivered;
4. at least two payroll parallel runs have completed without a material unresolved error;
5. the Client has accepted the asset and contract registers;
6. required employee consultation and consent steps are complete;
7. critical systems access is tested; and
8. no unresolved critical security or safety issue prevents transfer.

The JSC will assess readiness in good faith. Minor open items may be documented with owners and deadlines and will not unreasonably delay Transfer.

### 9.3 Employees

The Operator will offer transfer to eligible dedicated employees through lawful processes. The India Entity will offer terms that, in aggregate, are no less favorable immediately after transfer, recognize continuous service where legally permitted, and assume accrued leave and specified employee liabilities funded through the agreed settlement.

No individual is guaranteed to accept transfer. The Operator is not liable for refusal where it has complied with the agreed communication and transfer process. The Client decides which roles remain in scope, subject to law and consultation obligations.

### 9.4 Assets and contracts

Dedicated assets purchased with Client funds transfer for nominal consideration, subject to taxes. Shared assets may be purchased at net book value if agreed. Assignable dedicated vendor contracts will transfer with required consent; otherwise, the Parties will arrange a temporary subcontract or replacement.

### 9.5 Knowledge transfer and acceptance

The Operator will deliver process maps, standard operating procedures, control descriptions, open-item logs, vendor and asset registers, employee records, training sessions, and sixty days of post-transfer stabilization support.

Final acceptance occurs when the Transfer Register is signed and all critical items are delivered. Non-critical deficiencies will be listed for correction within thirty days.

## 10. Delayed or early transfer

The Client may request early transfer after 30 September 2028 on at least 120 days’ notice, provided Readiness Criteria are met and the remaining committed Operate fees through the original Transfer Date are paid at 50%.

If Transfer is delayed due primarily to Client or India Entity readiness, monthly management fees continue plus documented incremental costs. If delay exceeding ninety days is primarily caused by Operator breach, the management fee is reduced by 10% during the attributable delay.

## 11. Data protection and information security

The Parties will execute a data-processing schedule before personal data processing begins. The Operator will process Client data only for the Services and documented instructions, restrict access, encrypt data in transit and at rest where supported, maintain logging for privileged access, and require comparable safeguards from subprocessors.

A confirmed personal-data or security incident affecting Client systems or data will be notified within twenty-four hours. The Operator will preserve evidence, cooperate with investigation, and not notify third parties without authorization unless legally required.

Client payment systems and production customer data will not be accessed until the Client completes its security review and grants expressly scoped access.

## 12. Intellectual property

Client owns Client data and work product created by dedicated personnel in performing Client-directed product work, subject to valid assignments. Operator retains its pre-existing methodologies, templates, software, operating tools, and generalized know-how.

Upon full payment, Client owns bespoke operating documents created exclusively for the Transferred Operations. The Operator grants a perpetual internal-use licence to embedded Operator materials necessary to continue operations.

## 13. Confidentiality and publicity

Confidential Information will be protected using reasonable care and disclosed only on a need-to-know basis. Obligations last seven years; trade secrets and personal data remain protected as required by law. The Operator may not identify the Client as a customer or use its marks without written approval.

## 14. Compliance, audit, and records

Each Party will comply with anti-bribery, sanctions, employment, tax, and corporate laws applicable to its role. The Operator will maintain accurate records supporting charges and the Services for seven years.

Once annually, and additionally after a material incident, the Client may audit relevant controls on twenty business days’ notice. Audits must avoid unreasonable disruption and protect other customers’ information. The Client bears routine audit costs; the Operator bears reasonable re-audit costs where a material nonconformity is found.

## 15. Warranties

The Operator warrants professional and workmanlike performance, lawful employment administration, and authority to enter this Agreement. The Client warrants that its instructions, supplied materials, and functional activities comply with applicable law.

Except as stated, forecasts and targets are not guarantees. The Operator does not warrant a particular hiring volume, attrition rate, incentive, regulatory decision, or business outcome.

## 16. Indemnities

The Operator indemnifies the Client for third-party claims arising from the Operator’s unlawful employment of dedicated personnel, bodily injury caused by Operator negligence, or infringement by Operator-created materials. The Client indemnifies the Operator for claims arising from Client products, Client-directed regulated activities, Client materials, or unlawful instructions.

The indemnified Party must promptly notify the indemnifying Party, permit control of defense, and provide reasonable cooperation. No settlement may admit fault or impose non-monetary obligations on the indemnified Party without consent.

## 17. Liability

Neither Party is liable for consequential, exemplary, or punitive damages or lost profit, except amounts payable to third parties under an indemnity. Each Party’s aggregate liability is capped at the management and Build fees paid or payable in the twelve months preceding the event.

The cap does not apply to fraud, wilful misconduct, death or personal injury caused by negligence, employee payroll funds misapplied, confidentiality breach, data-protection breach, intellectual-property infringement, or liabilities that cannot be limited. Liability for confidentiality and data-protection breach is capped at two times the preceding twelve months’ fees.

## 18. Termination and step-in

Either Party may terminate material breach not cured within thirty days, reduced to ten days for non-payment. The Client may terminate for convenience after the first anniversary on 120 days’ notice and payment of committed costs, transition charges, and 35% of management fees otherwise due during the next six months.

For a serious service failure threatening business continuity, the Client may temporarily step in after notice, using Operator personnel and documentation solely to stabilize the Services. The Parties will agree reasonable safeguards and costs.

On expiry or termination, the Operator will provide up to six months’ exit assistance at then-current rates. Termination does not automatically transfer employees or contracts; lawful transfer processes remain required.

## 19. Disputes and governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Telangana, India. Executives will attempt resolution for twenty business days. Unresolved disputes will be finally resolved by three-arbitrator arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, seated in Hyderabad and conducted in English. Courts at Hyderabad may grant interim relief.

## 20. General

Neither Party may assign without consent, except Client may assign to the India Entity at Transfer and either Party may assign to a successor with adequate financial capacity. The Operator remains responsible for approved affiliates and subcontractors.

Force majeure excuses affected performance but not payment. If it continues for sixty days and materially prevents Services, either Party may terminate the affected scope.

This Agreement, its schedules, approved plans, and change orders form the entire agreement. In conflict, a signed change order prevails, then this Agreement, then schedules, then operating documents. Amendments must be signed. Electronic signatures and counterparts are valid.
