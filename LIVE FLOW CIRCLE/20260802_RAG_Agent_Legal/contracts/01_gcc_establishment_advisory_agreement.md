> **Synthetic demo document.** All companies, individuals, addresses, commercial terms, and events in this agreement are fictitious. This document is intended only for retrieval and knowledge-management demonstrations and is not legal advice.

The Consultant and Client are each a **Party** and together the **Parties**.

## 1. Background and objective

The Client develops enterprise workflow and observability software and is evaluating an India Global Capability Center (**GCC**) for product engineering, customer reliability, finance operations, and analytics. The Client wishes to make an evidence-based decision on location, operating model, legal structure, cost, risk, and implementation sequence.

The Consultant will assess the feasibility of a GCC initially accommodating 120 employees and capable of scaling to 300 employees within thirty-six months. The engagement is advisory. No recommendation constitutes a guarantee regarding talent availability, regulatory approval, tax outcome, real-estate availability, incentive award, or business performance.

## 2. Scope of services

### 2.1 Mobilization and discovery

The Consultant will conduct a kick-off workshop, establish a joint workplan, and collect information on the Client’s business strategy, target functions, skill profiles, security requirements, time zones, budgets, and decision criteria. The Consultant will interview up to eighteen Client stakeholders and review reasonable materials supplied through the agreed secure workspace.

### 2.2 Feasibility and business case

The Consultant will prepare a five-year business case comparing an India GCC with continued outsourcing and hiring in the Client’s existing markets. The model will address projected headcount, compensation, benefits, occupancy, technology, professional services, statutory compliance, travel, recruitment, transition, inflation, foreign-exchange sensitivity, and a reasonable contingency.

The business case will identify its assumptions and show base, upside, and downside scenarios. Figures are planning estimates, exclusive of taxes unless stated otherwise, and are not binding vendor quotations.

### 2.3 Location strategy

The Consultant will evaluate Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, and Chennai using agreed criteria including talent depth, attrition, compensation, office supply, connectivity, ecosystem maturity, business continuity, and relevant state policies. Two shortlisted cities will receive deeper analysis, and the final report will recommend a primary and secondary location.

The Consultant will facilitate up to eight exploratory discussions with recruiters, workspace providers, or ecosystem participants. The Consultant will disclose any referral fee connected with a recommended vendor.

### 2.4 Operating model and organization design

The Consultant will recommend a captive, hybrid, or staged operating model; define governance between headquarters and India; identify decision rights; and propose an initial organization structure. The design will cover a country leader, functional leaders, enabling teams, reporting lines, shared services, service measures, and escalation forums.

### 2.5 Establishment roadmap

The Consultant will develop an implementation roadmap covering incorporation, banking, registrations, interim employment options, recruiting, workplace, IT, security, payroll, finance, procurement, communications, and launch readiness. It will identify dependencies, indicative owners, decision gates, and critical-path risks.

### 2.6 Exclusions

Unless added through change control, the Services exclude legal opinions, tax opinions, statutory audits, incorporation filings, employee hiring, background checks, immigration processing, lease brokerage, architectural design, construction, technology deployment, payroll execution, and operation of the GCC.
