# Corporate Secretarial and Board Governance Services Agreement

> **Synthetic demo document.** This agreement uses invented organizations, people, addresses, dates and commercial terms solely for knowledge-retrieval demonstrations. It is not legal advice or an executable instrument.

This Agreement is made on 1 April 2026 between **Harborstone Governance Advisors LLP**, 18 Riverwalk Road, Pune 411001 (“Provider”), and **Velora Mobility India Private Limited**, 7 Innovation Park, Hinjawadi, Pune 411057 (“Company”). The Company is a wholly owned subsidiary of a fictitious German mobility-technology group and operates a Pune GCC.

## 1. Appointment

The Company appoints the Provider to deliver routine corporate secretarial and governance administration during the Term. The Provider accepts the appointment subject to the timely decisions and information of the Company’s board and shareholders. The Provider acts as an independent service provider and does not replace the directors’ statutory duties.

## 2. Included services

The annual service includes:

1. maintaining a compliance calendar for routine Companies Act filings;
2. maintaining electronic copies of the registers of members, directors, charges and contracts disclosed by the Company;
3. preparing agendas, notices, attendance records, draft minutes and standard resolutions for up to six board meetings and one annual general meeting;
4. coordinating the circulation and signing of board papers;
5. preparing routine annual returns and financial-statement filing forms based on Company-approved accounts;
6. recording one routine appointment or resignation of a director and one allotment of shares;
7. tracking director disclosures and director KYC due dates;
8. supporting routine significant-beneficial-ownership reporting based on information supplied by the parent group;
9. providing a quarterly compliance dashboard; and
10. responding to routine secretarial queries for up to ten advisory hours per quarter.

The statutory records remain the Company’s property and will be stored in the Provider’s encrypted document portal with access for nominated Company personnel.

## 3. Service levels

The Provider will issue draft board notices at least seven days before a scheduled meeting where it receives final agenda inputs ten business days before the meeting. Draft minutes will be delivered within ten business days after receipt of the meeting recording and action notes. Routine forms will be prepared at least five business days before the known statutory due date, provided the Company supplies complete inputs at least twelve business days before that date.

The quarterly dashboard will classify matters as complete, due within thirty days, overdue, or blocked by the Company. A missed service level requires a corrective plan but does not automatically invalidate a corporate action.

## 4. Company responsibilities

The Company shall:

- nominate a governance coordinator and ensure directors review materials;
- give complete and accurate information on ownership, related-party arrangements, contracts, borrowings and officer changes;
- provide approved financial statements and auditor details;
- hold meetings, exercise judgment and approve resolutions;
- sign and return forms before filing cut-offs;
- pay government filing fees and additional fees; and
- immediately notify the Provider of notices from regulators or any change affecting beneficial ownership.

Directors remain responsible for the Company’s affairs, disclosures and filings. The Provider may rely on signed confirmations without independent investigation.

## 5. Excluded and additional work

The service excludes litigation, legal opinions, securities offerings, mergers, business transfers, capital reductions, compounding of offences, foreign-exchange filings, tax advice, employment compliance, insolvency advice, secretarial audits and representation at investigations.

Additional meetings are charged at INR 35,000 each. Non-routine transactions require a written scope and estimate. Urgent work requested with fewer than three business days’ notice may carry a 20% priority surcharge.

## 6. Fees, invoicing and filing charges

The annual professional fee is **INR 1,440,000**, exclusive of taxes, invoiced quarterly in advance in equal instalments. Invoices are payable within thirty days. Government fees, stamp duty, certification fees, travel and courier expenses are charged separately. No expense above INR 20,000 will be incurred without approval except a mandatory filing fee needed to meet a deadline.

If an undisputed invoice remains unpaid for twenty days after notice, the Provider may suspend non-critical work. It will identify any imminent filing deadlines before suspension.

## 7. Governance process

The parties will hold a monthly compliance review. The Company’s Legal Director and the Provider’s Engagement Partner will meet quarterly to review upcoming transactions, blocked filings and legislative changes.

An urgent issue that may expose the Company or a director to penalty will be escalated within one business day. The Provider will describe the issue, deadline, required decision and known consequence. Business and legal decisions remain with the Company.

## 8. Filing dependencies and delayed instructions

The Provider is not responsible for a late or inaccurate filing caused by late, incomplete or inaccurate Company instructions, unavailable government portals, or a director’s failure to sign. When an input is late, the Provider will use reasonable efforts to mitigate the delay but may charge reasonable additional fees.

The Provider is responsible for penalties directly resulting from its failure to submit a complete, Company-approved filing by a notified deadline, limited to the governmental late fee for that filing. This remedy does not apply where the portal rejects a filing for reasons outside the Provider’s control.

## 9. Confidentiality and records

Each party shall keep the other party’s non-public information confidential during the Term and for five years thereafter. Trade secrets and personal data remain protected for so long as they retain that character. Permitted disclosures include those to professional advisers, affiliates and authorities on a need-to-know basis.

The Provider will maintain appropriate access controls, multi-factor authentication and daily backup for the records portal. A confirmed personal-data breach affecting Company records will be reported within forty-eight hours. At termination, the Provider will export the corporate records in commonly readable formats within fifteen business days after payment of undisputed fees.

## 10. Intellectual property

Company-specific statutory registers, approved minutes and filed forms belong to the Company. The Provider retains its templates, compliance logic, workflow tools and know-how. The Company receives a perpetual licence to use embedded template material as part of its completed records.

## 11. Representations and compliance

Each party represents that it has authority to enter into this Agreement. The Provider will deliver the services with reasonable professional care and use personnel holding required qualifications. Both parties shall comply with anti-bribery, sanctions and data-protection laws.

The Provider will disclose any actual conflict of interest. The Company acknowledges that the Provider may serve other GCCs, including companies in the mobility sector, provided it does not misuse confidential information.

## 12. Liability and indemnity

Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss. The Provider’s aggregate liability is capped at the fees paid in the preceding twelve months. The cap does not cover fraud, wilful misconduct, breach of confidentiality, or infringement of the other party’s intellectual property.

The Company indemnifies the Provider against claims arising from decisions made on materially inaccurate Company instructions. The Provider indemnifies the Company for third-party claims caused by the Provider’s fraud, wilful misconduct or unauthorised disclosure of Company confidential information.

## 13. Term, renewal and termination

The initial Term runs from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027 and renews automatically for successive one-year periods unless either party gives sixty days’ notice. Annual fees may increase at renewal by the lower of 6% or the year-on-year change in the India Consumer Price Index, unless the parties agree otherwise.

Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within fifteen days. The Company may terminate for convenience on thirty days’ notice. The Provider may terminate immediately if continued work would violate law or professional rules. Accrued fees, approved expenses and completed additional work remain payable.

## 14. Disputes and general

The parties will escalate disputes to the Company’s Chief Financial Officer and Provider’s Managing Partner for a fifteen-day resolution period. Unresolved disputes shall be arbitrated by one arbitrator in Pune, in English, under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

Neither party may assign this Agreement without consent, except to an affiliate in connection with a reorganisation that assumes all obligations. Force majeure excuses affected performance but not payment for completed work. This Agreement and signed statements of work constitute the entire agreement. Amendments must be in writing. Electronic signatures are valid.
