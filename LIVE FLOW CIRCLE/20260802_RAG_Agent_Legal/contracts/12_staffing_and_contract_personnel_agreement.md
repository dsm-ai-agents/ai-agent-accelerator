# India GCC Staffing and Contract Personnel Agreement

> **Synthetic demo document.** Every party, person, address and transaction is fictitious. It is supplied solely for knowledge-retrieval testing and must not be treated as legal advice or an executable agreement.

This Agreement is made on 15 March 2026 between Alderwave Financial Technologies plc (“Client”) and DeccanBridge Staffing Services Private Limited (“Supplier”), with Meridian Harbor GCC Advisory Private Limited acting only as GCC implementation coordinator (“GCC Adviser”).

## 1. Scope and term

Supplier will employ and assign contract personnel to the Client’s Hyderabad GCC for software engineering, test automation, finance operations and program support. The initial term is 24 months and renews for successive 12-month periods unless either party gives 60 days’ notice.

Assignments are initiated through written work orders stating role, skills, start date, location, monthly rate, shift, supervisor and expected duration. No minimum volume applies. The initial forecast is 65 personnel.

## 2. Employment responsibility

Supplier is the sole legal employer of assigned personnel and is responsible for recruitment, employment contracts, salary, provident fund, employee state insurance where applicable, professional tax, gratuity accrual, bonus, leave, insurance, payroll withholding and all required registers and returns. Supplier shall provide quarterly compliance certificates and supporting challans on request.

The Client controls project priorities, deliverables, workplace access and day-to-day task allocation, but shall not purport to dismiss personnel or change their employment terms. Performance or conduct concerns must be reported to Supplier, which will take lawful employment action.

## 3. Screening and onboarding

Supplier shall verify identity, address, education and the preceding five years of employment and conduct legally permissible criminal-record screening with written consent. Personnel may not access Client systems until screening and confidentiality documentation are complete. Exceptions require the Client security officer’s written approval.

The Client shall provide project orientation, system access and workplace safety information. Supplier shall ensure completion of security, anti-bribery, prevention-of-harassment and acceptable-use training.

## 4. Rates and invoicing

Indicative monthly rates, exclusive of GST, are:

| Role | Standard monthly rate |
|---|---:|
| Software Engineer | INR 265,000 |
| Senior Software Engineer | INR 350,000 |
| Test Automation Engineer | INR 245,000 |
| Finance Operations Analyst | INR 135,000 |
| Program Coordinator | INR 155,000 |

Rates include salary, statutory contributions, standard benefits, Supplier overhead and margin. Rates may increase once each renewal year by the lower of 6% or the documented increase in employment cost, subject to 45 days’ notice.

Invoices are based on approved monthly timesheets and payable within 30 days. The Client may dispute an item within 15 days but shall pay undisputed amounts. Supplier shall not withhold employee wages due to a Client invoice dispute.

## 5. Working time and overtime

Standard service is eight productive hours per Business Day, five days per week. Pre-approved work beyond 45 hours in a week is billed at 1.5 times the hourly equivalent; work on a declared Client holiday is billed at twice that equivalent or compensated by a day off if agreed. No overtime is payable without electronic approval from the relevant Client manager, except emergency work later ratified within two Business Days.

Supplier shall enforce statutory working-hour, rest-day and leave requirements. Night-shift transport and legally required safeguards are included only where the work order expressly states a night shift.

## 6. Timesheets, absence and leave

Personnel shall submit weekly timesheets. The Client must approve or reject them with reasons within three Business Days; otherwise they are deemed approved. Planned leave shall be coordinated with the Client, but Supplier retains responsibility for leave entitlement. Monthly rates are not reduced for up to two approved leave days per month. Longer absence is credited pro rata unless a substitute is supplied.

## 7. Performance and replacement

The Client may request removal for substantiated performance, misconduct, security, conflict or policy concerns. Supplier shall remove a critical security risk immediately and other personnel within five Business Days, subject to law. A qualified replacement will be proposed within ten Business Days for standard roles and 20 for specialist roles.

If personnel resign within 30 days of assignment or fail mandatory screening, Supplier will provide a replacement without onboarding charge. Supplier does not guarantee uninterrupted availability where the Client rejects qualified replacements or materially changes the role.

## 8. Permanent conversion

The Client may directly employ assigned personnel with their consent. If employment begins within the first six months of assignment, the conversion fee is 12% of annual fixed compensation; during months seven through twelve, 6%; after 12 completed months, no fee. No restriction prevents a person from accepting employment. Supplier shall cooperate with an orderly release after outstanding wages and notice requirements are addressed.

## 9. Client obligations

The Client shall provide a safe workplace, appropriate equipment, access limited to role needs, clear supervision and timely approvals. It shall not require unlawful work, discriminate, harass, solicit payroll information unrelated to the assignment or instruct personnel to process transactions beyond authorized controls. The Client will promptly report workplace injuries and security incidents.

## 10. Confidentiality, data and intellectual property

Supplier and personnel shall protect Client information during the Agreement and for five years afterward. Personal data shall be processed only for staffing administration, secured through role-based access and encryption, and reported as compromised within 24 hours of discovery.

All deliverables created by personnel specifically within an approved Client assignment are works commissioned for the Client and, upon payment, are assigned to the Client to the extent permitted by law. Supplier retains payroll systems, staffing methods and pre-existing materials. Personnel shall execute reasonable confirmatory assignments.

## 11. Security

Supplier shall maintain access reviews, device restrictions, incident procedures and annual awareness training. Personnel may use only Client-approved devices for production data. On removal or end of assignment, the Client disables access and Supplier returns badges and equipment within two Business Days. Lost assets are reimbursed at depreciated replacement value where caused by personnel negligence.

## 12. Audit

Once annually, and additionally after credible non-compliance evidence, the Client may inspect records directly relevant to wage payment, statutory remittances, screening and invoicing on ten Business Days’ notice. Audit access excludes unrelated employee data and Supplier margins. Supplier shall cure material compliance gaps promptly and reimburse reasonable audit cost if underpayment exceeds 5%.

## 13. Indemnities

Supplier indemnifies the Client for claims arising from failure to pay personnel, employment-status obligations as their employer, statutory remittance defaults, Supplier screening misconduct, or Supplier confidentiality and IP breach. The Client indemnifies Supplier for unsafe premises, unlawful Client instructions, discrimination by Client personnel, or materials and systems supplied by Client.

## 14. Liability and insurance

Neither party is liable for lost profits or indirect loss, except amounts payable to third parties under an indemnity. General aggregate liability is capped at fees paid in the preceding 12 months. The cap is twice that amount for confidentiality, data security and statutory employment defaults, and does not apply to fraud, wilful misconduct or unpaid wages.

Supplier shall maintain employer liability/workmen compensation, commercial general liability, professional indemnity and cyber insurance, each with limits commercially reasonable for the engagement.

## 15. Termination

Either party may terminate for uncured material breach after 30 days, insolvency, bribery or serious security breach. The Client may terminate a work order on 15 days’ notice and this Agreement on 60 days’ notice. The Client shall pay services through release, approved overtime and non-cancellable costs expressly listed in a work order.

Supplier shall manage lawful employee notice, redeployment or separation. For 30 days after termination it will provide timesheet, asset, access and personnel-transition reports. The GCC Adviser may coordinate but assumes no employment liability.

## 16. General and disputes

The parties are independent contractors. Supplier may not subcontract employer obligations without written consent. Neither party may use the other’s name publicly. Assignment to an affiliate is permitted with notice if obligations are preserved.

Disputes shall first be escalated to the parties’ operations heads, then executives. Unresolved disputes are subject to arbitration by one arbitrator seated in Hyderabad, in English. Telangana law governs, and Hyderabad courts may grant interim relief.
