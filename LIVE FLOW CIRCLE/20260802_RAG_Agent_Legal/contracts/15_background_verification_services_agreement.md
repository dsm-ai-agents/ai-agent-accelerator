# India GCC Candidate Background Verification Services Agreement

> **Synthetic demo document.** All parties, candidates, addresses and terms are fictitious and intended solely for contract-search demonstrations. This is not legal advice and must not be used for actual screening.

This Agreement is effective 1 May 2026 between Rheinspire Mobility India Private Limited (“Client”) and VerityMango Screening Private Limited (“Provider”). Meridian Harbor GCC Advisory Private Limited (“GCC Adviser”) coordinates the Client’s Gurugram GCC launch but neither orders checks nor determines candidate suitability unless expressly authorized.

## 1. Term and purpose

Provider shall conduct lawful, consent-based pre-employment and employment background checks requested by Client. The initial term is 12 months and renews annually unless either party gives 45 days’ notice.

## 2. Available screening packages

The following packages may be ordered through the approved portal:

| Package | Included checks | Fee per candidate |
|---|---|---:|
| Standard | Identity, address, two employments, highest education | INR 3,800 |
| Enhanced | Standard plus permissible court-record search and global sanctions | INR 6,200 |
| Finance Trust | Enhanced plus credit check where lawful and relevant | INR 7,400 |
| Executive | Enhanced plus directorship, media and three employments | INR 12,500 |

GST, university charges, overseas verification fees and approved translation costs are additional. Estimated annual volume is 600 Standard, 180 Enhanced, 40 Finance Trust and 20 Executive checks. No minimum volume is guaranteed.

## 3. Orders and permissible purpose

Client shall select a package appropriate to the role and provide candidate name, contact details, role, location and proposed start date. Checks may be ordered only for legitimate employment purposes and must be proportionate. Political beliefs, union membership, health, biometric or other sensitive checks are excluded unless separately assessed and legally authorized.

Provider shall not use deceptive pretexting, purchase unlawfully obtained databases or contact a current employer unless the candidate specifically permits it. Criminal and credit checks are limited to lawful sources and relevant roles.

## 4. Consent and notices

Before processing, Provider shall give the candidate a clear notice describing checks, data sources, purposes, recipients, retention, cross-border access and grievance contact, and obtain auditable affirmative consent. Client shall ensure that participation is not coerced beyond lawful employment requirements.

If consent is withdrawn before completion, Provider will stop checks that can reasonably be stopped and report the order as withdrawn. Client alone determines the employment consequence, subject to law.

## 5. Turnaround times

Target turnaround begins after complete documents and consent:

- identity and sanctions: two Business Days;
- Indian address: four Business Days;
- each employment: seven Business Days;
- Indian education: ten Business Days;
- permissible court-record search: seven Business Days; and
- overseas components: 20 Business Days.

The target for a complete Standard report is ten Business Days and Enhanced report is 12 Business Days. University closures, unresponsive former employers, inconsistent records, government-system outages and candidate delay pause the clock. Provider shall issue a status update every three Business Days for overdue components.

## 6. Classification of results

Reports use the following classifications:

- **Clear:** information verified without material discrepancy;
- **Review:** discrepancy or inability to verify that may require context;
- **Adverse Information:** credible information materially inconsistent with supplied information or relevant legal records; and
- **Insufficient:** required evidence remains unavailable.

Provider reports facts and source limitations; it shall not recommend hiring or rejection. Minor date differences of up to 30 days and commonly used name variations are not adverse absent other concerns.

## 7. Adverse findings and candidate response

Before a final report containing Adverse Information is released, Provider shall conduct reasonable quality review. Client must allow the candidate an opportunity to explain or correct the information before making a materially adverse employment decision. On request, Provider shall share the relevant report excerpt and source description, unless prohibited by law or protective order.

Disputes raised by a candidate will be acknowledged within two Business Days and reinvestigated within ten Business Days where feasible. The report will be marked disputed until resolved. Client retains sole responsibility for relevance, proportionality, fairness and the final decision.

## 8. Fees and invoicing

Provider invoices monthly for initiated checks. A check cancelled before external work begins is not charged; after work begins, completed components and committed third-party fees are payable. Invoices are due in 30 days. Fees may increase on renewal by up to 5% with 60 days’ notice.

Provider shall obtain written approval before incurring any overseas third-party charge above INR 2,500 for one candidate.

## 9. Accuracy and quality

Provider shall use trained personnel, documented procedures and credible sources and shall distinguish verified facts from unresolved allegations. It warrants faithful transcription of source responses but cannot guarantee source accuracy or availability.

Provider’s monthly quality target is 99% reports without Provider-caused material error. A confirmed material error will be corrected promptly and the affected check credited. Aggregate monthly service credits are capped at 10% of that month’s fees.

## 10. Data security

Provider shall use encryption in transit and at rest, multifactor authentication, least privilege, quarterly access review, endpoint protection, secure deletion and annual security testing. Candidate reports may be delivered only through the approved portal; ordinary email attachments are prohibited.

Provider shall notify Client within 12 hours after discovering a suspected incident involving Client candidate data and provide facts, containment status, affected records and remediation updates. Provider will preserve evidence and cooperate with lawful notification decisions.

## 11. Data location, subprocessors and transfers

Primary candidate records shall be hosted in India. Provider may use disclosed verification partners in another country only where an overseas check is ordered or a source is located there, candidate notice covers the transfer, and contractual security obligations apply. New material subprocessors require 30 days’ notice; Client may object on reasonable privacy or security grounds.

Provider remains responsible for subcontracted work. Investigators shall receive only the minimum data necessary for their component.

## 12. Retention and deletion

Provider will retain completed reports for 180 days after delivery and then delete or irreversibly anonymize them, except unresolved disputes, legal holds or mandatory law. Consent evidence and audit logs may be retained for three years. Client controls its own retention and should restrict reports to authorized HR, legal and security personnel.

On termination, Client may export reports still within retention. Provider will certify deletion after applicable periods.

## 13. Confidentiality

Candidate data, reports, Client hiring plans, pricing and security information are confidential. Each party may disclose them only to authorized personnel, advisers under duty, regulators or as legally compelled. Confidentiality continues for five years, while personal data remains protected for its full lifecycle.

Provider may use aggregated, irreversibly anonymized statistics to improve turnaround and quality but not to build candidate marketing profiles.

## 14. Client responsibilities

Client shall limit checks to authorized roles, provide accurate order details, secure portal credentials, restrict report access, avoid automated rejection based solely on a Provider label, and meet applicable candidate-notice and fairness obligations. Client shall not circulate reports to unrelated affiliates or future employers.

The GCC Adviser may receive aggregate progress dashboards but not candidate-level reports unless the candidate notice and Client authorization expressly permit it.

## 15. Audit and compliance

Provider shall comply with applicable privacy, anti-bribery and employment-screening requirements and maintain incident, consent, access and deletion records. Client may audit relevant controls annually on 15 Business Days’ notice or after a material incident. Audits must protect other customers and avoid access to raw third-party databases.

## 16. Indemnity and liability

Provider indemnifies Client for third-party claims caused by checks conducted without required consent, unlawful source methods, Provider security breach or material report falsification. Client indemnifies Provider for disproportionate check instructions, discriminatory or unlawful employment decisions, unauthorized report disclosure by Client, or inaccurate Client-supplied information.

Neither party is liable for indirect loss. General liability is capped at fees paid in the preceding 12 months. Liability for privacy, confidentiality or security breach is capped at three times that amount. Fraud, wilful misconduct and payment obligations are not capped.

## 17. Termination

Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within 20 Business Days, insolvency, bribery, repeated unauthorized checks or material security failure. Client may terminate for convenience on 45 days’ notice. Provider shall finish or safely cancel open orders as directed, subject to candidate rights and payable committed costs.

## 18. General and disputes

The parties are independent contractors. Provider may not assign the Agreement except to a successor acquiring substantially all relevant business and meeting security requirements. Amendments require signature. Force majeure extends affected deadlines, but Provider must continue reasonable data protection.

Disputes first go to the service managers and then executives for 15 Business Days. Unresolved disputes shall be arbitrated in Gurugram by one arbitrator, in English, under Indian arbitration law. Haryana law governs.
