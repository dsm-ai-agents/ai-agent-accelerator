# Integrated Facilities Management and Physical Security Agreement

> **Synthetic demo document.** All companies, individuals, locations, prices and circumstances are invented for a contract knowledge-base demonstration. This is not legal advice and is not intended for execution.

This Agreement is effective 1 January 2027 between Sentinel Grove Facilities Private Limited (“Provider”) and PrairieStone Agritech India Private Limited (“Client”) for Client’s 72,000-square-foot global capability centre in Gurugram.

## 1. Services

Provider shall deliver integrated facilities management and physical security, including:

- helpdesk and work-order coordination;
- housekeeping and washroom services;
- preventive and corrective maintenance of Client-controlled equipment;
- reception, mailroom and visitor management;
- security guarding and patrols;
- access-card administration;
- consumable and pantry inventory;
- pest control and waste coordination;
- environmental, health and safety support;
- emergency preparedness and drills; and
- vendor access and permit-to-work administration.

Landlord-controlled structure, lifts, central chillers, external façade and common-area systems remain outside Provider’s maintenance responsibility. Provider shall coordinate issues with the landlord and track them through closure.

## 2. Staffing

Provider shall maintain the following minimum deployment:

| Role | Day shift | Evening shift | Night shift |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Facility manager | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| Facility executives | 2 | 1 | 0 |
| Technicians | 3 | 2 | 1 |
| Housekeeping personnel | 18 | 8 | 3 |
| Security supervisors | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Security officers | 8 | 6 | 5 |
| Receptionists | 3 | 1 | 0 |

Provider shall ensure statutory weekly rest and relief coverage without reducing the minimum deployment. Roles may be adjusted through quarterly demand review. Critical security posts may not be left unmanned.

Personnel shall be trained, uniformed, identifiable and background verified to the extent lawful. Armed guards are not included.

## 3. Operating hours

The facility operates 24x7. Reception is staffed 7:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. on working days. Helpdesk requests may be logged at all times. The facility manager is available during business hours and on call for critical incidents.

Provider shall publish duty rosters one week in advance and maintain an escalation list. Absence at a critical post must be covered within two hours.

## 4. Preventive maintenance

Within 30 days, Provider shall establish an asset register and preventive-maintenance calendar for UPS units, distribution boards within Client areas, supplemental cooling, fire extinguishers, access-control devices, CCTV equipment, pantry equipment, pumps and other assigned assets.

Provider shall complete at least 95% of scheduled preventive maintenance in the due month and 100% of statutory or safety-critical activities. Missed safety activity shall be escalated immediately.

Provider may perform routine adjustments and minor repairs using included labour. Parts, specialist services and individual repairs above INR 25,000 require Client approval except immediate work necessary to prevent injury or major property damage, capped at INR 100,000 per event.

## 5. Incident priorities

| Priority | Description | Acknowledge | Attend site | Target restoration |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Critical | Fire/life safety issue, major flooding, total Client-area power loss, active intrusion | 5 minutes | 10 minutes | 2 hours or continuous containment |
| High | Loss of cooling to critical room, access-control degradation, major hygiene issue | 15 minutes | 30 minutes | 4 hours |
| Medium | Local equipment or service failure affecting multiple users | 1 hour | 2 hours | 1 business day |
| Routine | Minor repair, setup or consumable request | 4 business hours | 1 business day | 3 business days |

Provider shall maintain incident logs and issue a preliminary report within four hours for critical incidents. A root-cause and corrective-action report is due within three business days for Provider-controlled events.

## 6. Physical security

Provider shall operate access-control monitoring, reception screening, patrols, key management, visitor records, material movement passes and incident escalation under Client-approved procedures.

Visitors must be preregistered or approved by an authorised Client host, present acceptable identification and wear a visible badge. Provider shall not copy government identification unless law or a documented Client policy requires it. Visitor records are retained for 180 days.

Security officers may inspect bags only in accordance with a published, non-discriminatory policy and applicable law. They may use reasonable measures to protect people and property but have no authority to interrogate, detain unlawfully or access Client systems.

Provider shall immediately contact emergency services and Client security when there is credible risk to life, suspected crime, forced entry or serious threat.

## 7. CCTV and access data

Provider shall monitor Client-owned CCTV at authorised security points. Cameras shall cover entrances, exits, delivery areas and defined common zones, not restrooms, wellness rooms or private meeting spaces.

CCTV footage shall be retained for 90 days and access logs for 365 days unless Client requires preservation for investigation or law. Export requires documented approval and an evidence chain. Provider shall notify Client within one hour of unauthorised access, deletion or disclosure.

## 8. Housekeeping, hygiene and waste

Provider shall clean work areas daily, high-touch common surfaces at least three times during occupied hours, washrooms at least hourly, and perform deep cleaning quarterly. Cleaning chemicals shall be labelled, safely stored and accompanied by safety data sheets.

Provider shall segregate general, recyclable, electronic and sanitary waste and use Client-approved licensed vendors where required. No confidential paper may leave the site except through secure destruction. Provider shall supply monthly waste and recycling metrics.

## 9. Health, safety and emergency management

Provider shall maintain an emergency response plan aligned with building procedures, train wardens, inspect evacuation routes weekly and coordinate two evacuation drills per year. It shall maintain first-aid kits and ensure trained first-aiders are present on each shift.

Provider shall investigate occupational incidents involving its personnel and cooperate on Client incidents. Fatality, hospitalisation, fire, electrical shock or material environmental release must be reported to Client immediately and in writing within one hour.

Provider shall administer permits for hot work, work at height, confined space and electrical isolation. No high-risk work may begin without approval and controls.

## 10. Business continuity

Provider shall maintain relief personnel, alternate suppliers for critical consumables and emergency contact lists. It shall participate in one annual Client continuity exercise and one tabletop security exercise.

During transport disruption, epidemic restriction or local emergency, Provider shall prioritise security posts, essential hygiene, critical maintenance and emergency response. Reduced non-critical service requires Client approval where feasible and daily reporting.

## 11. Consumables and third-party purchases

The monthly fee includes ordinary cleaning materials, waste bags, washroom paper products and pantry consumables up to INR 250,000 monthly. Excess requires approval. Food, beverages, plants, décor, event services, capital equipment and landlord charges are excluded.

Provider shall obtain three quotations for non-emergency purchases above INR 100,000 unless Client approves single-source procurement. Provider shall disclose rebates and may not retain undisclosed vendor commissions.

## 12. Fees and payment

Client shall pay INR 2,100,000 per month plus taxes. The base contract value for 36 months is INR 75,600,000. Fees increase 6% on each anniversary, primarily reflecting statutory wage changes. A further adjustment is permitted only for a demonstrated mandatory wage increase exceeding the built-in escalation.

The fee includes baseline staffing, supervision, uniforms, training, ordinary tools and included consumables. Approved parts, specialist vendors and extraordinary event staffing are reimbursable at cost plus 5%.

Invoices are payable within 30 days with staffing attendance, service report, SLA calculation and reimbursable evidence.

## 13. Service levels and credits

Provider shall achieve 98% critical-post staffing, 95% preventive-maintenance completion, 95% critical/high incident attendance, at least 90% work-order closure within target, and monthly hygiene audit scores of at least 92%.

For each missed target, Client receives 1.5% of the monthly fee, capped at 10%. Any unmanned critical security post lasting over 30 minutes creates an additional INR 50,000 credit per event. Credits do not apply to Client-directed vacancies, landlord-controlled delay or force majeure, provided Provider documents mitigation.

Three consecutive months below a target require a formal cure plan and may constitute chronic failure.

## 14. Governance and reporting

Provider shall hold weekly operations meetings and monthly performance reviews. Reports shall include attendance, incidents, maintenance, work orders, visitor volumes, access exceptions, safety observations, energy and waste metrics, complaints, training, spending and improvement actions.

Client may inspect services and records. Provider shall implement reasonable corrective actions within agreed dates. Meridian Harbor GCC Advisory may participate in quarterly reviews as Client’s operating-model adviser but has no authority to amend this Agreement.

## 15. Personnel obligations

Provider is the sole employer of its personnel and is responsible for wages, benefits, supervision, statutory contributions, uniforms and discipline. No Provider person becomes a Client employee. Provider shall indemnify Client against employment claims caused by Provider’s non-compliance.

Provider shall prohibit harassment, discrimination, alcohol or illegal drugs on site. Client may request immediate removal for substantiated misconduct, security risk or serious performance concern; Provider shall supply a qualified replacement.

## 16. Confidentiality and privacy

Provider may encounter floor plans, employee information, visitor data, security procedures and business information. It shall use such information only for services, restrict access and train personnel in confidentiality.

Provider may not photograph Client areas, disclose occupancy patterns or use Client’s name in marketing without consent. Confidentiality survives five years; security plans and trade secrets remain protected while sensitive.

Personal data shall remain in Client-approved systems. Provider shall not export it outside India or use it for analytics unrelated to the services.

## 17. Subcontracting

Specialist maintenance, pest control and waste removal may be subcontracted to approved vendors. Core security supervision may not be subcontracted without written consent. Provider remains liable for all subcontractors and shall verify licences and insurance.

## 18. Insurance and liability

Provider shall maintain worker coverage, commercial general liability of INR 100,000,000, fidelity/crime cover of INR 20,000,000 and professional indemnity appropriate to services.

Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss. Provider’s aggregate liability is capped at 125% of fees paid or payable during the preceding 12 months. The cap excludes fraud, wilful misconduct, confidentiality or privacy breach, employment liabilities, theft or deliberate property damage by Provider personnel, and death or personal injury.

## 19. Term and termination

The initial term is 36 months and renews annually unless either party gives 90 days’ notice. Either party may terminate material breach after 30 days’ cure. Client may terminate immediately for corruption, serious safety misconduct, deliberate security compromise, persistent failure to pay personnel, or insolvency.

Client may terminate for convenience after month 12 on 90 days’ notice without penalty. Provider shall continue safe services during notice.

## 20. Exit assistance

For up to 60 days, Provider shall transfer asset registers, maintenance history, keys, access records, vendor contacts, permits, open work orders and procedures; support incoming-vendor familiarisation; and remove its property and credentials. Provider shall not withhold Client records due to a fee dispute.

## 21. Disputes and general

The parties shall escalate disputes to their contract executives for 15 business days. Unresolved matters shall be decided by a sole arbitrator seated in Gurugram, in English, under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. Courts in Gurugram may issue interim orders.

Provider may not assign without consent. Amendments require authorised signatures. Force majeure excuses affected performance but not accrued payment, and the affected party shall mitigate and report.
