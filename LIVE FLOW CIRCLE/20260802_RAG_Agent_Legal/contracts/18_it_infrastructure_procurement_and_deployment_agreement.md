# IT Infrastructure Procurement and Deployment Agreement

> **Synthetic demo document.** Every entity, person, address, specification and commercial term is fictitious and supplied only for knowledge-retrieval demonstrations. This document is not legal advice and must not be used as an actual procurement contract.

This Agreement is effective 10 August 2026 between Blue Circuit Technologies Private Limited (“Supplier”) and LumenArc Retail Platforms India Private Limited (“Client”). Meridian Harbor GCC Advisory Private Limited (“GCC Advisor”) shall monitor programme status for Client but is not a purchaser, guarantor or reseller.

## 1. Project objective

Supplier shall procure, configure, deliver and deploy end-user and network infrastructure for Client’s new 320-person product and data hub in Chennai. Supplier shall provide only new, genuine equipment obtained from authorised distribution channels and shall pass through all manufacturer warranties.

## 2. Deliverables

The initial bill of materials includes:

| Component | Quantity | Minimum specification |
|---|---:|---|
| Engineering laptops | 220 | 32 GB RAM, 1 TB encrypted SSD, three-year onsite warranty |
| Standard laptops | 120 | 16 GB RAM, 512 GB encrypted SSD, three-year onsite warranty |
| 27-inch monitors | 680 | QHD, height-adjustable |
| USB-C docks | 340 | 90W power delivery |
| Wireless access points | 28 | Wi-Fi 6E, centrally managed |
| Access switches | 14 | 48-port PoE+, redundant power |
| Edge firewalls | 2 | High-availability pair and three-year security subscription |
| Meeting-room kits | 12 | Camera, microphones, controller and display integration |
| Asset tags | 400 | Tamper-evident QR code linked to Client asset ID |

Exact manufacturer models are listed in an approved purchase order. Supplier may not substitute a model without Client’s written approval. A substitution must provide equal or better specifications, maintain interoperability and not increase price.

## 3. Milestones

Supplier shall complete:

1. final design and device build standard by 21 August 2026;
2. delivery of core network equipment by 15 September 2026;
3. installation and testing of core network by 25 September 2026;
4. first 200 configured laptops by 30 September 2026;
5. remaining equipment by 15 October 2026; and
6. final acceptance and documentation by 23 October 2026.

Time is material because Client’s first employee cohort starts 5 October 2026. Supplier shall provide a twice-weekly delivery dashboard showing purchase-order status, serial numbers, logistics risks and corrective action.

## 4. Client dependencies

Client shall provide approved architecture, naming convention, software licences, device-management enrolment credentials, security policies, usable workspace, power and connectivity. Client shall make authorised personnel available for decisions and testing.

Supplier is entitled to a milestone extension only to the extent a documented Client dependency causes critical-path delay. Supplier must notify the impact within two business days and take reasonable mitigation action.

## 5. Configuration and security

Supplier shall install the approved operating-system image, BIOS settings, endpoint management agent, endpoint protection, disk encryption and standard applications. No shared administrative credential may remain after handover. Supplier shall transfer privileged credentials through Client’s approved secrets-management process and delete all copies.

Configuration work shall occur in a segregated staging zone. Supplier shall prevent unauthorised removable media, maintain an activity log and restrict access to named screened personnel. Supplier may not copy Client source code or production data onto deployment devices.

Any confirmed loss, theft, tampering or unauthorised access involving Client equipment or credentials must be reported within two hours.

## 6. Delivery, title and risk

Supplier bears packing, freight, transit insurance, duties and delivery risk until equipment is physically delivered and counted at Client’s Chennai site. Title transfers after Client pays the relevant equipment invoice, but risk transfers only on delivery.

Supplier shall record manufacturer, model, serial number, MAC address where applicable, purchase-order number, warranty dates and Client asset ID. The final asset register shall be supplied in CSV and human-readable PDF.

Supplier may make partial deliveries with Client’s consent. Client is not required to accept visibly damaged, counterfeit, incorrectly specified or undocumented equipment.

## 7. Acceptance testing

Client has ten business days after a milestone delivery to conduct tests. Acceptance criteria include quantity reconciliation, power-on test, specification check, network connectivity, enrolment into device management, encryption validation, approved software load and asset-register accuracy of at least 99.5%.

Client shall issue acceptance or a rejection report identifying material failures. Supplier shall correct failures within five business days and resubmit without additional charge. Items not rejected within the test period are deemed accepted, except latent defects, warranty claims, security backdoors or fraud.

Failure of more than 5% of a batch constitutes batch rejection at Client’s option.

## 8. Fees and payment

The maximum contract value is INR 68,450,000 plus applicable taxes:

- 20% advance against a bank guarantee;
- 40% after delivery of core equipment and 200 laptops;
- 30% after full physical delivery; and
- 10% after final acceptance and documentation.

Prices are firm and include procurement, freight, staging, configuration, deployment, packaging removal, initial training and project management. Statutory tax changes after signature may be passed through with evidence. Foreign-exchange changes are Supplier’s risk.

Client may retain payment for rejected items and may set off agreed service credits. Undisputed invoices are payable within 30 days.

## 9. Delay credits and cover purchase

For Supplier-caused delay, Supplier shall credit 0.5% of the delayed milestone value per completed week, capped at 10% of that milestone. If a milestone is more than 20 business days late, Client may cancel undelivered items and procure alternatives. Supplier shall reimburse the reasonable excess cover cost, capped at 15% of the cancelled item value.

Credits do not apply to force majeure or documented Client delay. They do not prevent termination for persistent material breach.

## 10. Warranty and support

Supplier warrants for 12 months after final acceptance that configuration and installation services conform to the Agreement and accepted design. Supplier shall correct service defects within three business days.

Manufacturer hardware warranties shall be at least three years for laptops, switches, firewalls and wireless access points, and one year for monitors and accessories. Supplier shall coordinate warranty claims throughout those periods even if this project term has expired.

For laptops that fail within 30 days of acceptance, Supplier shall provide a new replacement, not a refurbished unit, within two business days in Chennai. Thereafter, Supplier shall provide a loaner within one business day if repair exceeds two business days.

## 11. Change control

Any change to quantity, specification, site, milestone or price requires a written change order stating impact. Supplier shall not act on an oral instruction. Emergency security configuration may be authorised by Client’s Chief Information Security Officer by email and documented within two business days.

## 12. Personnel and subcontractors

Supplier shall use suitably qualified, background-verified personnel. Client may require removal of personnel for documented security or conduct concerns. Supplier remains responsible for logistics, configuration and installation subcontractors and shall disclose any subcontractor with access to Client premises or systems.

No work or data may be transferred outside India without written approval.

## 13. Intellectual property

Each party retains pre-existing intellectual property. On payment, Client owns project-specific configuration scripts, rack diagrams, build documentation and asset records created exclusively for the project. Supplier retains general methods and reusable tools but grants Client a perpetual licence necessary to operate the delivered environment.

Supplier warrants that supplied products and services do not knowingly infringe third-party rights. Supplier shall defend and indemnify Client against claims caused by unauthorised or counterfeit equipment or Supplier-created deliverables.

## 14. Confidentiality and data protection

Security architecture, credentials, serial-number registers, network diagrams, prices and business plans are Client Confidential Information. Supplier shall use them only for performance, restrict access by need and return or securely delete them within 30 days after completion.

Supplier shall comply with applicable Indian data-protection law for personnel and contact data. It shall notify Client within six hours of a suspected compromise and preserve relevant logs.

## 15. Audit and records

Supplier shall retain purchase evidence, authorised-distributor records, serial-number records and acceptance documentation for seven years. Client may audit once annually and after credible counterfeit, fraud or security concerns. Audits shall minimise disruption; Supplier shall bear audit cost if a material discrepancy exceeding 2% is found.

## 16. Liability and insurance

Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages. Supplier’s aggregate liability is capped at 125% of contract value. The cap does not apply to fraud, wilful misconduct, confidentiality or security breach, IP indemnity, counterfeit goods, personal injury or property damage.

Supplier shall maintain commercial general liability, professional indemnity, cyber insurance and transit cover, each appropriate to the project and with general liability of at least INR 100,000,000.

## 17. Termination and transition

Either party may terminate an uncured material breach after 20 business days’ notice. Client may terminate immediately for counterfeit goods, deliberate security compromise, corruption or insolvency. Client may terminate remaining scope for convenience on 30 days’ notice, paying accepted goods, non-cancellable evidenced commitments and completed services, but no lost profit.

On termination, Supplier shall deliver paid equipment, configuration records, credentials, warranties and current asset data and provide up to 80 hours of transition assistance at agreed rates.

## 18. Disputes and general

The parties shall escalate disputes to their project sponsors for ten business days. Unresolved disputes shall be arbitrated by a sole arbitrator seated in Chennai, in English, under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. Chennai courts may grant interim relief.

Supplier may not assign without Client’s consent. Notices shall be sent by courier and email. This Agreement, purchase orders, approved design and change orders form the entire agreement; conflicting purchase-order boilerplate has no effect.
