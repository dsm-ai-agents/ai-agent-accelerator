# India Private Limited Company Incorporation Services Agreement

> **Synthetic demo document.** All companies, persons, addresses, identifiers, amounts, and events in this agreement are fictitious and are provided solely for RAG demonstration and testing. This is not legal advice and is not intended for execution.

This India Private Limited Company Incorporation Services Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into on 12 January 2026 by and between **CedarBridge Corporate Services Private Limited**, a fictitious company having its office at 44 Museum Crescent, Bengaluru 560001 (“Provider”), and **Northstar Robotics Inc.**, a fictitious Delaware corporation having its office at 680 Beacon Avenue, Boston, Massachusetts 02110 (“Client”).

## 1. Background and objective

The Client intends to establish a wholly owned Indian subsidiary to operate a robotics engineering and product-support Global Capability Center (“GCC”) in Bengaluru. The Provider will coordinate incorporation and the initial registrations described below. The proposed entity name is **Northstar Robotics India Private Limited**, subject to approval by the competent authority.

## 2. Scope of services

The Provider shall:

1. conduct an initial entity-setup workshop and issue a document checklist;
2. perform a preliminary name-availability review and submit up to two name-reservation applications;
3. coordinate applications for digital signature certificates and director identification numbers for two proposed directors;
4. prepare draft charter documents using the Client-approved objects and capital structure;
5. prepare and submit incorporation forms and supporting declarations;
6. coordinate issuance of the certificate of incorporation, permanent account number and tax deduction account number;
7. assist with Goods and Services Tax, Karnataka professional tax and Shops and Commercial Establishments registrations where applicable;
8. supply a digital statutory records starter pack, including initial registers and draft first-board-meeting materials; and
9. provide reasonable administrative support for opening one operating bank account with a scheduled commercial bank selected by the Client.

The Provider may use licensed company secretaries, chartered accountants and filing agents. The Provider remains responsible for coordinating their work but does not control governmental or bank decisions.

## 3. Deliverables and target timetable

Within five business days after the Effective Date, the Provider will deliver the entity-setup checklist and responsibility matrix. The name application is targeted for submission within three business days after receipt of the preferred names and business objects. Incorporation is targeted within twenty business days after receipt of complete, notarised and apostilled documents.

Tax and establishment registrations are targeted within thirty business days after incorporation. The bank-account assistance workstream is targeted for completion within forty-five business days after incorporation. These are planning targets, not guaranteed approval dates.

## 4. Client dependencies

The Client shall timely provide:

- certified constitutional documents and evidence of registered address;
- board approval for the Indian investment and authorised signatories;
- passport, address, tax residency, photograph and contact details for each proposed director and beneficial owner;
- notarisation, apostille or consular legalisation where requested;
- the proposed business objects, share capital, ownership structure and remittance plan;
- evidence of lawful occupation of the Indian registered office;
- responses to governmental, professional-adviser and bank know-your-customer queries; and
- accurate information concerning sanctions, politically exposed persons and beneficial ownership.

The Client warrants that supplied information is complete and accurate. Any target date moves day-for-day for Client delay. The Provider may pause work if required records are missing, inconsistent or reasonably suspected to be unlawful.

## 5. Registered office and directors

The Client will procure the registered office. The Provider may introduce a third-party registered-office vendor, but occupation charges and the underlying licence are outside this Agreement. The Client will nominate at least two directors and ensure that the board satisfies Indian residency requirements. The Provider does not act as nominee shareholder or director and does not guarantee the continued availability of any introduced individual.

## 6. Fees and expenses

The fixed professional fee is **INR 925,000**, exclusive of applicable taxes, payable as follows:

- 40% on signing;
- 35% upon submission of incorporation forms; and
- 25% upon delivery of the incorporation and registration pack.

Government charges, stamp duty, notarisation, apostille, translation, courier, registered-office fees, bank charges and capital contribution are reimbursable at cost. The Provider must obtain written approval before incurring any single unbudgeted third-party expense above INR 25,000.

Invoices are due within fifteen days. Overdue undisputed sums bear interest at 1% per month. The Client may dispute an invoice within seven business days, identifying the disputed item and basis, and shall pay the undisputed portion when due.

## 7. Change control

The fee assumes one Indian subsidiary, two directors, one corporate shareholder and authorised capital not exceeding INR 10,000,000. Additional shareholders, regulated activities, foreign-national employment permissions, more than two name-reservation attempts, restructuring, or resubmission caused by changed Client instructions requires a written change order.

## 8. No guarantee; professional limitations

The Provider will perform the services with reasonable skill and care. Government authorities and banks retain sole discretion over approvals, processing time and additional information. The Provider does not guarantee company name approval, incorporation by a particular date, tax registration, banking approval, investment incentives or any tax outcome.

This engagement does not include a legal opinion, foreign-exchange advice, transfer-pricing analysis, employment documentation, immigration advice, lease negotiation, intellectual-property registration, or ongoing secretarial compliance. Separate specialists should be retained for those matters.

## 9. Records, confidentiality and data protection

Each party shall protect non-public business, financial, personal and technical information received from the other and use it only for this engagement. Disclosure is permitted to personnel, advisers, banks and authorities with a need to know, subject to appropriate duties of confidence, or where required by law.

The Provider may process director and beneficial-owner personal data to conduct identity verification and make regulatory filings. Such data will be retained for eight years following completion, or longer if required by law, and then securely deleted. The Provider will notify the Client without undue delay and in any event within forty-eight hours after confirming unauthorised access to Client personal data in its control.

## 10. Intellectual property

Upon payment, the Client may use the completed entity-specific forms, registers and board materials for its internal corporate purposes. The Provider retains ownership of templates, checklists, methods and general know-how. Neither party acquires rights in the other’s names or marks.

## 11. Compliance and ethical conduct

Each party shall comply with applicable anti-bribery, sanctions, anti-money-laundering and data-protection laws. The Provider will not make facilitation payments. A request for an unofficial payment will be escalated to the Client’s designated legal contact. The Provider may immediately suspend services where continued performance could violate law.

## 12. Liability

Neither party is liable for indirect, consequential or punitive damages, loss of opportunity, or loss of anticipated savings. The Provider’s aggregate liability arising from this Agreement will not exceed the professional fees paid, except that the cap does not apply to fraud, wilful misconduct, breach of confidentiality, or liability that cannot lawfully be limited.
