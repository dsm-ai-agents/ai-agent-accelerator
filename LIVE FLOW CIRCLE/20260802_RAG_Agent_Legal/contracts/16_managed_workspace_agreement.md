# Managed Workspace Agreement

> **Synthetic demo document.** All entities, people, addresses, amounts and events in this agreement are fictitious. This document is intended only for retrieval-augmented generation and contract-search demonstrations and is not legal advice or an enforceable instrument.

This Managed Workspace Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into on 15 May 2026 and is effective from 1 June 2026 (“Effective Date”) by Cedar Square Workspaces Private Limited (“Provider”) and Northstar Clinical Systems India Private Limited (“Client”). Meridian Harbor GCC Advisory Private Limited (“GCC Advisor”) coordinated the workspace selection and implementation but is not liable for either party’s performance except as expressly stated in a separate agreement.

## 1. Purpose and premises

The Client is establishing a healthcare-technology global capability centre in Bengaluru. Provider shall deliver a secure, ready-to-occupy managed office on the seventh and eighth floors of the fictitious Cedar Square One building, Outer Ring Road, Bengaluru (“Premises”). The Premises may be used for software engineering, analytics, finance, human resources and related administrative activities, but not clinical testing, manufacturing, public retail or storage of hazardous materials.

## 2. Term and lock-in

The Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for 36 months unless terminated earlier. The first 24 months constitute the “Lock-in Period.” Client may not terminate for convenience during the Lock-in Period. After it expires, Client may terminate on 120 days’ written notice.

If Client exits for convenience during the Lock-in Period, it shall pay an early-exit charge equal to the recurring workspace fee for the unexpired portion of the Lock-in Period, less any fees Provider actually receives from a replacement occupant for the same dedicated area. Provider shall use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate the charge.

The parties may renew for successive 12-month periods only by written agreement signed at least 90 days before expiry.

## 3. Capacity and dedicated areas

Provider shall make available:

| Item | Included quantity |
|---|---:|
| Dedicated workstations | 200 |
| Executive cabins | 6 |
| Eight-person meeting rooms | 6 |
| Sixteen-person boardroom | 1 |
| Interview rooms | 4 |
| Secure server/network room | 1 |
| Wellness and first-aid rooms | 2 |
| Dedicated storage lockers | 40 |

The monthly fee assumes 200 assigned users and permits up to 220 persons on-site at one time. Additional seats are subject to availability and cost INR 43,500 per seat per month. A request for more than 25 additional seats requires 60 days’ notice. Provider does not guarantee contiguous expansion space unless confirmed through a signed change order.

## 4. Mobilisation and acceptance

Provider shall complete furniture installation, access-control configuration, signage, network-room readiness and fire-safety checks by 25 May 2026. Client may inspect the Premises during the following three business days. A defect prevents acceptance only if it materially impairs safe occupation or more than 10% of workstations. Minor defects shall be recorded in a punch list and corrected within ten business days.

The Premises are deemed accepted upon the earliest of written acceptance, productive occupation by 25 Client personnel, or five business days after handover without a material rejection notice. Acceptance does not waive latent-defect or safety claims.

## 5. Included services

The recurring fee includes ergonomic furniture, electricity during building hours, generator backup for critical systems, HVAC, daily housekeeping, reception, common-area maintenance, drinking water, pantry equipment, basic consumables, pest control, visitor management, access cards, CCTV coverage of common access points and meeting-room scheduling.

Provider shall supply two shared 1 Gbps internet connections from different carriers for ordinary business use. These shared links are not dedicated circuits and carry no application-performance warranty. Client-specific MPLS, SD-WAN, leased lines, static public IP addresses, cross-connects, penetration testing and telecom usage charges are excluded. Provider shall facilitate installation by a Client-selected carrier and provide building access at no additional coordination charge.

## 6. Service standards

Provider shall operate reception and standard facility support from 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. Monday through Friday, excluding notified public holidays. Building access shall be available 24x7 to authorised Client personnel.

Critical incidents involving complete power loss, fire-safety impairment, loss of all access control or flooding of the dedicated area require acknowledgement within 15 minutes and active remediation within 30 minutes. High-priority HVAC or shared-internet failures require acknowledgement within 30 minutes. Other requests require acknowledgement within four business hours.

If the dedicated area is unusable for more than four consecutive business hours due solely to Provider-controlled causes, Client receives a credit of 2% of the monthly recurring fee for each additional four-hour block, capped at 15% per month. Credits are Client’s financial remedy for service-level failure, except for confidentiality breaches, gross negligence, wilful misconduct or physical injury.

## 7. Charges, deposit and taxes

Client shall pay INR 3,300,000 per month plus applicable taxes. Fees increase by 6% on each anniversary of the Effective Date. The stated contract value of INR 118,800,000 excludes escalation, taxes, dedicated connectivity, additional seats and approved change orders.

Client shall maintain an interest-free refundable security deposit of INR 9,900,000. Provider may deduct undisputed unpaid charges or documented repair costs beyond ordinary wear. The balance shall be refunded within 45 days after complete vacation, access-card return and final reconciliation.

Invoices are payable within 30 days. Client may withhold a disputed amount if it gives particulars within ten business days and timely pays the undisputed balance. Overdue undisputed sums accrue interest at 1% per month.

## 8. Access, security and conduct

Provider shall issue access credentials only from Client-authorised lists. Client shall notify joiners, movers and leavers promptly and is responsible for activity under credentials it fails to deactivate. Lost cards cost INR 1,500 each. Visitors must be preregistered, show identification and remain escorted in Client areas.

Provider may retain access-control logs for 180 days and common-area CCTV footage for 90 days unless preservation is legally required. Cameras shall not be installed inside meeting rooms, wellness rooms or restrooms.

Client shall comply with building safety rules, occupancy limits and anti-harassment requirements. Provider may deny entry to a person presenting an immediate safety or security risk, with prompt notice to Client’s workplace lead.

## 9. Data and confidentiality

Each party shall protect the other’s non-public commercial, security and technical information with reasonable care and use it only for this Agreement. Provider personnel may not access Client devices, files or network traffic except with documented authorisation or for an emergency necessary to protect life or property.

Provider shall notify Client within four hours after confirming unauthorised access to Client’s dedicated area, access logs or visitor records. Confidentiality survives five years; trade secrets remain protected while legally qualifying as trade secrets.

## 10. Maintenance and alterations

Provider is responsible for base-building systems and normal maintenance of supplied furniture and fixtures. Client is responsible for its computers, specialised equipment and damage caused by its personnel or vendors.

No drilling, cabling, signage, high-load equipment or structural change is permitted without prior approval, not to be unreasonably withheld. Approved alterations shall comply with law and building standards. Unless otherwise agreed, Client shall remove its alterations and restore affected surfaces on exit.

Provider may enter dedicated areas for maintenance on 24 hours’ notice. No notice is required for emergencies, provided Provider records the entry and informs Client promptly.

## 11. Business continuity

Provider shall maintain generator capacity for life-safety systems, common areas and contracted workstation power, test the generator monthly and conduct an annual evacuation drill. Provider shall maintain an alternate temporary workspace arrangement for up to 40 seats if the Premises are unusable for more than five business days due to a Provider-controlled event. Alternate space is subject to security review and may be within 15 kilometres.

Provider does not warrant uninterrupted operation during city-wide utility, carrier or government restrictions outside its reasonable control. It shall nevertheless activate continuity procedures and provide daily status reports.

## 12. Insurance and liability

Provider shall maintain public liability insurance of at least INR 100,000,000 per occurrence and insurance for its employees and property. Client shall insure its equipment and business interruption risks.

Neither party is liable for indirect, consequential or lost-profit damages. Each party’s aggregate liability is capped at fees paid or payable during the preceding 12 months. The cap does not apply to fraud, wilful misconduct, death or personal injury, confidentiality breach, damage to tangible property, or Client’s payment obligations.

## 13. Termination

Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within 30 days after notice, or immediately for insolvency, unlawful use of the Premises or a serious unremedied safety threat. Provider may suspend access for undisputed fees overdue more than 30 days only after a further ten-business-day warning and reasonable executive escalation.

Upon expiry or termination, Client shall vacate, remove property, return credentials and provide forwarding details. Provider shall allow a ten-business-day managed decommissioning window. Property left for more than 30 days after notice may be stored at Client’s cost.

## 14. General

Neither party may assign this Agreement without consent, except to an affiliate or successor acquiring substantially all relevant business, provided the assignee can perform. Provider may subcontract housekeeping, security and maintenance but remains responsible for subcontractors.

Disputes shall first be escalated to operational leads and then executives. Unresolved disputes shall be finally resolved by a sole arbitrator seated in Bengaluru under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. Proceedings shall be in English. Courts in Bengaluru have jurisdiction for interim relief.

This Agreement, its schedules and signed change orders constitute the entire agreement. Amendments must be in writing. Notices shall be delivered by recognised courier and email to the designated legal contacts.
