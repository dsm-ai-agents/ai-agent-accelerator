## Summary

User training is Phase 7 of NexaFlow onboarding and typically happens in week 5 of a standard 4–6 week enterprise implementation. This document describes role-based training tracks, NexaFlow Academy certifications, the champions program, change management practices and adoption metrics tracked during the 30-day hypercare period and beyond.

## Main Content

### Training Included by Plan

| Plan | Included Training |
|---|---|
| Starter | Self-paced NexaFlow Academy courses |
| Professional | Academy + 2 live virtual sessions (admin and builder) |
| Enterprise | Full role-based training program, train-the-trainer, champions program |

On-site custom workshops are available for $4,000 per day (see `pricing_guide_v3.md`).

### Role-Based Training Tracks (Enterprise)

| Track | Audience | Format | Duration | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Platform Admin | IT and CoE admins | Live virtual + labs | 2 × 3-hour sessions | Manage users, roles, SSO, connectors, audit logs |
| Flow Builder | Business analysts, automation team | Live + hands-on project | 3 × 3-hour sessions | Build, test and publish flows |
| Agent Builder | Advanced builders | Live + labs | 2 × 3-hour sessions | Configure AI agents, knowledge sources, thresholds |
| Operator | Teams handling exceptions and queues | Live virtual | 1 × 90 minutes | Work queues, approvals, dashboards |
| Approver / Viewer | Managers and approvers | 10-minute video + quick guide | Self-paced | Approve in Slack/Teams/email, read dashboards |

Training uses the customer's own workflows from Staging, not generic demos, so users practise on processes they will actually run.

### Training Schedule Within Onboarding

- **Week 2:** Platform Admin training (so IT can configure SSO and roles).
- **Weeks 2–3:** Flow Builder training for the customer's automation team (they build alongside NexaFlow).
- **Week 5:** Operator and Approver training, close to go-live so knowledge is fresh.
- **Go-live week:** floor support and open "office hours" sessions.

For complex 8–12 week projects, operator training is delivered in waves by business unit or region.

### NexaFlow Academy Certifications

| Certification | Requirement |
|---|---|
| NexaFlow Certified Admin | Course + 40-question exam |
| NexaFlow Certified Builder | Course + build project reviewed by NexaFlow |
| NexaFlow Certified Agent Designer | Builder certification + agent project |

Enterprise customers receive 10 free certification vouchers per year.

### Champions Program

Each department nominates one or two champions who:
- attend extra training and a monthly champions call,
- act as first-line support for colleagues,
- collect improvement ideas for new workflows,
- share wins in internal channels.

Customers with active champions reach target adoption about 30% faster than those without.

### Change Management Practices

1. **Explain the why** — the executive sponsor announces the change and the expected benefits before training.
2. **Start small** — launch with 2–3 high-value workflows rather than everything at once.
3. **Remove the old path** — retire the legacy spreadsheet or email process shortly after go-live so users do not work in two places.
4. **Celebrate early wins** — share hours saved and faster cycle times from Command Center dashboards.
5. **Listen** — run a feedback survey at day 14 and day 30.

### Adoption Metrics

| Metric | Target by Day 30 | Target by Day 90 |
|---|---|---|
| Licensed users active weekly | 70% | 85% |
| Workflows in production vs planned | 100% of phase 1 | Phase 2 started |
| Exceptions resolved within SLA | 90% | 95% |
| Approvals completed in Slack/Teams/email | 60% | 80% |
| AI agent override rate | Below 15% | Below 10% |

### Post-Launch Support and Customer Success

- **Hypercare (first 30 days after go-live):** daily check-ins in week 1, then twice weekly; priority defect handling; weekly adoption report.
- **Hypercare exit review:** adoption metrics reviewed and handover to the named Customer Success Manager.
- **Quarterly business reviews:** ROI, adoption, roadmap and next workflows to automate.
- **Health score:** combines usage, support tickets and executive engagement to flag at-risk accounts.

## Key Facts

- Training is Phase 7, usually week 5 of a standard enterprise implementation.
- Enterprise gets role-based tracks for admins, builders, agent builders, operators and approvers.
- Approvers need only a 10-minute video; operators a 90-minute session.
- Adoption target: 70% weekly active users by day 30, 85% by day 90.
- Hypercare lasts 30 days after go-live, then CSM handover and QBRs.
