## Main Content

### General Questions

**Q: Is there a free trial?**
Yes. Starter and Professional offer a 14-day free trial with up to 5 users and 1,000 workflow runs. Enterprise does not have a self-serve trial; instead, a paid 60-day pilot can be arranged and credited against the first-year subscription.

**Q: Which currencies do you bill in?**
USD, EUR, GBP, INR and AUD. Non-USD prices are set by region each July and are not recalculated daily based on exchange rates.

**Q: What payment methods are accepted?**
Credit card for Starter and Professional monthly plans. Annual plans and all Enterprise contracts can pay by bank transfer or ACH with Net 30 terms.

**Q: Are taxes included?**
No. Prices exclude VAT, GST and sales tax, which are added based on the billing address.

**Q: What happens if we exceed our workflow run allowance?**
You receive alerts at 80% and 100%. Professional and Enterprise continue running and are billed overage ($6 and $4 per extra 1,000 runs respectively). Starter flows pause at 120% of the allowance.

**Q: Can we switch from monthly to annual billing?**
Yes, at any time. The annual price (about 17% lower) starts from the switch date.

**Q: Is there a discount for paying annually?**
Yes. Annual billing is roughly 17% cheaper than monthly billing. 2-year and 3-year terms receive an additional 5% and 10% respectively. See `discount_policy.md`.

**Q: Do approvers or viewers need to pay?**
Approvers are free. Viewers are free up to three times the number of Full Users on Professional and Enterprise. See `licensing_model.md`.

**Q: How much does implementation cost?**
Starter is self-serve and free. Professional Quick Start is $5,000 (optional). Enterprise implementation is $15,000 for the standard 4–6 week program or $35,000 for complex 8–12 week projects.

**Q: Will our price go up at renewal?**
Enterprise renewal increases are capped at 7% per year (5% on some 3-year contracts). Starter and Professional renew at the list price in effect at renewal.

**Q: Why did prices change in July 2026?**
Pricing Guide v3 added more workflow runs, unlimited AI agents on Enterprise and Agent Studio document extraction on Professional. Prices were raised to reflect the expanded AI capabilities, while included usage roughly doubled.

### Plan-Choice Questions

**Q: We have 80 users but need SCIM. Which plan?**
SCIM is Enterprise-only, so Enterprise is required, and the 100-user minimum applies.

**Q: We have 40 users and need SSO. Which plan?**
Professional includes SSO.
