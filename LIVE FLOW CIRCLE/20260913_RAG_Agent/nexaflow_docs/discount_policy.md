## Summary

This policy defines the discounts NexaFlow sellers can offer and who must approve them. Standard discounts (annual billing, multi-year terms) are built into `pricing_guide_v3.md` and need no approval. Additional discretionary discounts follow an approval matrix by seniority. The policy also covers special programs, prohibited practices and how discounts interact with renewals.

## Main Content

### 1. Standard Discounts (No Approval Required)

| Discount | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Annual billing | ~17% vs monthly price | Already reflected in annual list prices ($29 / $59 / $95) |
| 2-year term | 5% off annual price | Paid annually in advance |
| 3-year term | 10% off annual price | Paid annually in advance |

Standard discounts are stackable with each other only in the sense that the multi-year percentage applies to the annual price. They do not count toward the discretionary limits below.

### 2. Discretionary Discount Approval Matrix

Discretionary discount is measured as the percentage below the **annual list or tier target price** after standard discounts.

| Additional Discount | Approver | Turnaround |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10% | Account Executive (self-approved, logged in CRM) | Immediate |
| 10.1–20% | Sales Manager | 1 business day |
| 20.1–30% | VP Sales | 2 business days |
| Above 30% | CFO + VP Sales | 5 business days; deal desk review required |

For Enterprise deals, the tier floor in `enterprise_pricing.md` corresponds roughly to the 10% Sales Manager threshold.

### 3. Implementation Fee Discounts

- Quick Start (Professional, $5,000): up to 50% off with Sales Manager approval.
- Enterprise Standard Implementation ($15,000): up to 25% off with Sales Manager approval.
- Enterprise Complex Implementation ($35,000): **not discountable**.
- Implementation fees may never be fully waived for Enterprise.

### 4. Special Programs

| Program | Discount | Eligibility |
|---|---|---|
| Nonprofit | 15% | Registered nonprofit; Starter and Professional only |
| Education | 15% | Accredited schools and universities |
| Startup | 25% for year 1 | Under 50 employees, less than $10M funding, Starter or Professional |
| Competitive displacement | Up to 15% for year 1 | Replacing a named competitor contract; VP Sales approval |
| Case study partner | Up to 5% | Customer agrees to a public case study within 6 months of go-live |

Special program discounts count toward the discretionary matrix, so a nonprofit receiving 15% plus another 10% totals 25% and needs VP Sales approval.

### 5. End-of-Quarter Discounts

End-of-quarter discounts are allowed only in exchange for a clear commitment (signature by a set date, larger user count or longer term). Offering unconditional "quarter-end" discounts trains customers to wait and is not permitted.

### 6. Renewal Rules

- Discretionary discounts apply to the initial term only unless the contract states otherwise.
- At renewal, pricing moves back toward list, subject to the renewal cap (7% default for Enterprise).
- Customers on legacy Pricing Guide v2 prices move to v3 pricing at renewal with the renewal cap applied. See `pricing_faq.md` for historical prices.

### 7. Prohibited Practices

- Side letters or verbal discount promises not in the order form.
- Free months added to the end of a contract without approval (treated as a discount).
- Discounting overage rates below Enterprise committed-block pricing.
- Splitting one customer into multiple small contracts to avoid approval thresholds.
- Sharing internal floors or this policy with customers.

### 8. How to Request Approval

1. Create a discount request in the CRM opportunity.
2. Include deal size, term, competitor involved, and what the customer gives in return.
3. Attach the ROI summary.
4. Approvals expire after 30 days if the deal has not closed.

### Worked Example

A Professional customer with 120 users wants a 2-year contract and 15% extra discount.
- Annual list: $59 → 2-year standard discount 5% → $56.05.
- 15% discretionary → $47.64 per user/month → requires **Sales Manager** approval (10.1–20% band).

## Key Facts

- Annual billing (~17%) and multi-year discounts (5% / 10%) need no approval.
- AEs can give up to 10%; Sales Managers up to 20%; VP Sales up to 30%; above 30% needs the CFO.
- Complex Enterprise implementation fees are not discountable.
- Nonprofit and education discounts are 15%.
- Unconditional quarter-end discounts are prohibited.
