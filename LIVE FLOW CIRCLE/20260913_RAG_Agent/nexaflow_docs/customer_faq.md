## Main Content

### About the Product

**What does NexaFlow do?**
NexaFlow is a workflow automation and AI operations platform. It connects your business systems, automates multi-step processes and uses governed AI agents for tasks like classification, document extraction and summarisation. (Source: `product_overview.md`)

**Who is NexaFlow for?**
Mid-sized and enterprise organisations, typically 500–10,000 employees, in manufacturing, financial services, healthcare, retail and professional services.

**Do we need developers to use it?**
No. Business analysts build flows in the visual Flow Builder. Developers can extend the platform through the REST and GraphQL APIs.

**Which systems do you integrate with?**
More than 200, including Salesforce, SAP S/4HANA, Oracle NetSuite, Workday, ServiceNow, Microsoft 365, Slack and Snowflake. On-premise systems connect through NexaFlow Relay on Enterprise. (Source: `integration_guide.md`)

**Is there an API?**
Yes — REST and GraphQL APIs, webhooks, and Python and JavaScript SDKs.

**Can NexaFlow be installed on-premise?**
No. NexaFlow is cloud-only, with single-tenant and data residency options for Enterprise.

### AI

**How do the AI agents work?**
Agents are configured in Agent Studio with instructions, permitted tools and knowledge sources. They work inside workflows and can require human approval.

**Is our data used to train AI models?**
No. Customer data is never used for model training, and LLM providers operate with zero data retention.

**Can we control which AI models are used?**
Enterprise customers can restrict agents to an approved model list or bring their own LLM key.

### Security and Compliance

**Is NexaFlow secure?**
Yes. NexaFlow is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified, GDPR compliant, and offers HIPAA BAAs on Enterprise. (Source: `security_overview.md`)

**Do you support SSO and role-based access?**
SSO (SAML/OIDC) is available on Professional and Enterprise. Role-based access control is available on all plans, with custom roles on Enterprise.

**Do you provide audit logs?**
Yes, on Professional (90 days) and Enterprise (1 year, extendable to 7 years, with SIEM streaming).

**Where is our data stored?**
AWS in the US, EU or APAC depending on plan and choice.

**Who owns our data?**
You do. Data can be exported at any time and is deleted within 30 days of contract end.

### Implementation and Support

**How long does implementation take?**
Starter customers are up and running in days. A standard enterprise implementation typically takes 4–6 weeks; complex projects take 8–12 weeks. (Source: `implementation_timeline.md`)

**What does the onboarding process look like?**
Discovery, solution design, environment setup, integration configuration, data migration, testing, training, go-live and 30 days of post-launch support. (Source: `onboarding_guide.md`)

**What support do you offer?**
Email support on Starter, email and chat on Professional, and 24/7 phone, chat and email with a named Customer Success Manager on Enterprise.

**What is your uptime commitment?**
99.5% on Professional and 99.9% on Enterprise.

**Do you provide training?**
Yes. All customers get NexaFlow Academy; Enterprise customers receive a role-based training program. (Source: `training_and_adoption.md`)

### Pricing and Contracts

**How is NexaFlow priced?**
Per user per month, with three plans: Starter, Professional and Enterprise. Annual billing is cheaper than monthly billing. For current prices see `pricing_guide_v3.md` or ask your account executive.

**Is there a free trial?**
Yes, 14 days on Starter and Professional. Enterprise customers can run a paid 60-day pilot credited against the first year.

**Do approvers need a paid license?**
No. People who only approve requests via email, Slack or Teams do not need a paid license. (Source: `licensing_model.md`)

**What contract lengths are available?**
Monthly or annual for Starter and Professional; 1, 2 or 3 years for Enterprise.

### Results

**What results do customers see?**
Examples include a 40% reduction in manual work at a manufacturer, 35% faster processing at a financial services firm, 20 hours saved per week at a consulting firm and 30% fewer errors at a retailer. Typical payback is 6–9 months.

**Can we speak to a reference customer?**
Yes. References in your industry can be arranged at the proposal stage.
