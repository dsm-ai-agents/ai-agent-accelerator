## Slide 2 — Executive Summary
ABC Electronics, a fast-growing online consumer electronics retailer, currently handles ~300 customer queries a day across WhatsApp, email, and web with 8 full-time agents — resulting in 4–6 hour response times, no after-hours coverage, and limited visibility into support performance. We recommend deploying an AI Customer Support Agent (FAQ automation, order tracking, returns handling, WhatsApp/web channels, human escalation, and an analytics dashboard) delivered over a 6-week implementation. Expected outcome: sub-5-minute response times, 80% automated resolution, a 30% reduction in manual support workload, and 24/7 coverage — with industry benchmarks pointing to ROI payback in 3–6 months.

## Slide 5 — Proposed AI Solution
- **AI FAQ Agent** — automatically resolves routine, repetitive customer questions across channels.
- **Order Tracking Assistant** — gives customers instant, self-serve order status updates.
- **Returns & Refund Assistant** — guides customers through returns/refunds without agent involvement.
- **Human Escalation Workflow** — automatically routes complex or sensitive cases to a human agent.
- **Website Chatbot & WhatsApp AI Assistant** — brings AI support to the client's two highest-volume channels.
- **Daily Support Analytics Dashboard** — gives managers real-time visibility into volume, response time, and automation rate.

## Slide 6 — Solution Architecture
**Flow:** Customer → Website / WhatsApp / Email → AI Agent → Knowledge Base → Business Systems (order & returns data) → Human Agent (if escalation required)

The AI Agent sits in front of all three existing channels, queries a central knowledge base for FAQ/order/returns content, integrates with backend business systems for real-time order and returns data, and escalates only complex cases to the human team — preserving a single, trackable conversation thread per customer regardless of channel.
