Discovery Meeting Transcript

Client: ABC Electronics

Consultant: Thank you for meeting with us. Could you describe your current customer support process?

Client: We sell consumer electronics online. We receive around 300 customer queries every day through WhatsApp, email, and our website.

Consultant: How many support agents do you have?

Client: Eight full-time agents.

Consultant: What are the biggest challenges?

Client:
- Slow response times (4–6 hours)
- Repetitive questions
- Agents spend too much time answering FAQs
- No support after business hours
- Difficult to track conversations across channels

Consultant: What are your goals?

Client:
- Respond within 5 minutes
- Offer 24/7 support
- Reduce repetitive workload
- Improve customer satisfaction
- Escalate only complex cases to humans
